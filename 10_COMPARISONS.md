# 10 — Comparisons (Within Categories)

No fake numeric scores. Comparisons reflect evidence quality, practical fit, and risk—not marketing preference.

---

## Breathing

| Protocol | Best for | Evidence | vs peers |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Cyclic sighing | Mood + respiratory rate downshift | B (head-to-head RCT arm winner) | Beat matched mindfulness on positive affect/RR in Balban 2023; HR/HRV not superior |
| Resonance/~6 bpm | Trainable HRV/RSA, chronic regulation | B (mechanistic + clinical HRVB literature) | Stronger autonomic mechanism story than box; needs longer practice than a sigh |
| Exhale-emphasized no-holds | Hold-intolerant users | B (mechanism aligned) | Safer default than 4-7-8/box for many |
| Diaphragmatic | Dysfunctional chest breathing | B | Technique skill; often combined with pacing |
| Box breathing | Structured attention under pressure | C | Useful ritual; not best mood protocol in Balban 2023 |
| 4-7-8 | Memorable bedtime cue | C | Ratio mnemonic > unique physiology proof |
| Alternate nostril | Traditional ritual / paced breathing | D | Limited rigorous science; label traditional |
| Cyclic hyperventilation | Activation (caution) | C (comparator) | Higher risk; not for stress calm |

**Takeaway:** For stress/mood, prefer cyclic sighing or resonance/exhale-paced breathing. Treat holds as optional, not magical.

## Sleep & recovery

| Protocol | Best for | Evidence | Caveat |
|----------|----------|----------|--------|
| Stimulus control | Conditioned insomnia | A | Clinical regimen |
| Sleep restriction | Low sleep efficiency insomnia | A | Clinician-guided; safety risks |
| Morning light | Circadian anchoring | A | Timing matters |
| Fixed wake time | Irregular schedules | B | Foundational |
| Caffeine cutoff / evening dim light | Pharmacologic/circadian hygiene | B | Necessary but rarely sufficient alone |
| Brief nap | Alertness after restriction | B | Conflicts with insomnia stimulus control |
| Jet lag light+melatonin | Time-zone travel | B | Easy to mistime |
| Wind-down | Pre-sleep arousal | C | Hygiene-ish; weak alone |
| Yoga Nidra/NSDR | Deep rest / adjunct | C | **Not sleep replacement** |
| PMR / body scan | Somatic/cognitive arousal | B | Helpful adjuncts |

**Takeaway:** Chronic insomnia → CBT-I components (A). NSDR ≠ unpaid sleep debt.

## Acute vs chronic stress

| Acute tools | Chronic tools |
|-------------|---------------|
| Physiological sigh, grounding, brief PMR, TIPP/cold face, tactical breath | Exercise (A), sleep regularity + light (A/B), resonance breathing (B), mindfulness packages (B), social support (B), BA (A) |

Acute tools change state; chronic tools change load and capacity. Do not expect 2-minute sighs to fix sleep debt + inactivity.
