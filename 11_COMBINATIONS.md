# 11 — Combinations (Logical Stacks)

Stacks are sequencing heuristics with rationale—not guaranteed synergies. Stop any step that increases distress unsafely.

---

## Stack A — Acute stress spike (3–7 min)

1. **Affect label** (name it)  
2. **1–3 physiological sighs** or **5-4-3-2-1** if breath-averse  
3. **Brief PMR** shoulders/jaw  
4. **One if–then** for next action  

**Rationale:** Labeling + exhale downshift + somatic release + behavioral recommitment. Avoid hyperventilation.

---

## Stack B — Extreme emotional arousal (TIPP bridge)

1. **Temperature (cold face)** if medically appropriate  
2. **Intense exercise burst** OR paced exhale breathing  
3. **Progressive relaxation**  
4. Later: **reappraisal** or **opposite action** when intensity <7/10  

**Rationale:** Change body first (DBT crisis logic), cognition second.

---

## Stack C — Sleep tonight (non-chronic first aid)

1. **Caffeine cutoff already set** (or water only now)  
2. **Dim lights 60+ min**  
3. **Wind-down** (no bed work)  
4. **PMR or cyclic sighing in chair**  
5. Bed only when sleepy; if awake ~20 min → **stimulus control leave-bed**  
6. Optional short **NSDR** earlier in evening as rest—not as sleep substitute  

**Rationale:** Circadian + arousal + conditioning. Do not add more in-bed thinking tools.

---

## Stack D — Chronic insomnia (product framing)

**Clinician-supported CBT-I core:** stimulus control + sleep restriction titration + cognitive elements; adjunct relaxation.  
**Not a DIY stack for aggressive restriction.**

---

## Stack E — Perform in 15

1. Light movement + (if morning) outdoor light  
2. 2 min breath reset (sigh or short box)  
3. Centering + process cue  
4. PETTLEP of opening actions  
5. Locked PPR  
6. Obstacle if–then  

**Rationale:** Arousal → attention → imagery → automatic routine → contingency plan.

---

## Stack F — Goal week setup (20–30 min weekly)

1. **Values compass** (optional quarterly)  
2. **WOOP** for top wish  
3. **2–3 if–then plans**  
4. **Habit stack** for the first micro-behavior  
5. Midweek: **self-distancing** review of stuck point  

**Rationale:** Motivation (WOOP) + automation (II) + tiny cue (stack) + adaptive reflection.

---

## Stack G — Mind won't stop (daytime)

1. Postpone worry (book the slot)  
2. Resonance or exhale breathing 5–10 min  
3. Reappraisal or defusion for sticky content  
4. Tiny BA action  
5. Attention restoration if fatigued  

**Rationale:** Contain → downshift → reconstrual → approach behavior → restore attention.

---

## Stack H — Chronic stress load (lifestyle)

1. **Exercise most days** (A)  
2. **Fixed wake + morning light** (A/B)  
3. **Evening dim + caffeine policy** (B)  
4. **Resonance breathing 10 min daily** (B)  
5. **Social micro-connection** (B)  
6. Optional MBSR-style practice (B)  

**Rationale:** Highest-leverage load reducers first; breath/mindfulness as amplifiers, not sole pillars.

---

## Anti-stacks (avoid)

- Hyperventilation + cold water immersion unsupervised  
- Sleep restriction DIY + high-risk driving week  
- Outcome-only manifestation visualization + no if–then  
- Long in-bed meditation/NSDR while practicing stimulus control poorly (can become another incompatible bed behavior if it keeps you awake in bed—prefer chair)
