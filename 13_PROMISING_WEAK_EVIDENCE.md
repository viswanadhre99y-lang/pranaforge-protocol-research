# 13 — Promising but Weak / Mixed Evidence

Protocols or claims that are interesting enough to track, but should be labeled **preliminary**, **mixed**, or **indirect** in product copy.

---

## Cyclic sighing beyond the 2023 RCT

**What is promising:** Head-to-head remote RCT favors cyclic sighing for positive affect and respiratory rate vs mindfulness.  
**Why weak/limited:** Single key trial context (healthy adults, remote, WHOOP, 28 days); HR/HRV null; acute 1–3 sigh effects less directly tested.  
**Grade retained:** B for daily 5-min practice; acute spikes extrapolated.

---

## Yoga Nidra / NSDR as “sleep replacement”

**What is promising:** Small trials show subjective sleep improvements, some PSG changes, local-sleep-like EEG features while often behaviorally awake; feasibility in insomnia-reporting adults.  
**Why weak:** Heterogeneous methods; mixed vs quiet rest; small samples; **does not establish equivalence to sleep’s physiological functions.**  
**Honest framing:** Deep rest / adjunct recovery.

---

## Box breathing as superior calm tool

**What is promising:** Popular, structured, used in performance settings.  
**Why weak:** In Balban 2023, not the top mood protocol; limited isolating RCTs.  
**Honest framing:** Attention scaffold; optional holds.

---

## 4-7-8 exact ratio

**What is promising:** Memorable; extended exhale likely helpful.  
**Why weak:** Sparse high-quality trials of the exact Weil ratio; effects likely general slow-breathing.  
**Honest framing:** Counted exhale practice, not uniquely validated physiology.

---

## Alternate nostril breathing

**What is promising:** Traditional use; some small autonomic studies.  
**Why weak:** Study quality often low; traditional mechanism claims non-scientific.  
**Grade:** D.

---

## 5-4-3-2-1 grounding mnemonic

**What is promising:** Clinically ubiquitous; sensible attention competition.  
**Why weak:** Few rigorous RCTs of this exact script.  
**Honest framing:** Practical grounding skill; evidence mostly clinical practice + related grounding research.

---

## TIPP / opposite action as solo protocols

**What is promising:** Core DBT skills; strong package evidence for DBT in relevant populations.  
**Why weak:** Isolated skill RCTs limited.  
**Honest framing:** Skills from an evidence-based package—not the same as delivering DBT.

---

## Habit stacking (Clear)

**What is promising:** Matches cue-based habit theory and if–then planning.  
**Why weak:** Popular book > direct branded RCTs; routine vs time cues not clearly superior in some trials—repetition matters.  
**Honest framing:** Popular science wrapper on stronger II/habit science.

---

## Pomodoro Technique

**What is promising:** Practical timeboxing.  
**Why weak:** Limited rigorous trials of the branded 25/5 method.  
**Honest framing:** Structure heuristic.

---

## Autogenic training

**What is promising:** Long clinical tradition; some meta-analytic benefit signals.  
**Why weak:** Heterogeneous older literature; variable quality.  
**Honest framing:** Optional traditional self-relaxation method.

---

## Nature micro-breaks (ART)

**What is promising:** Multiple cognitive studies; coherent theory.  
**Why weak:** Mixed effect sizes; mechanisms debated; urban constraints.  
**Honest framing:** Promising fatigue antidote, not cure-all.

---

## Melatonin for everything sleep

**What is promising:** Jet lag / some circadian uses.  
**Why weak/overextended:** Not a general chronic insomnia cure; timing/dose matter; quality varies.  
**Honest framing:** Chronobiotic tool with specific indications—not generic “sleep vitamin.”
