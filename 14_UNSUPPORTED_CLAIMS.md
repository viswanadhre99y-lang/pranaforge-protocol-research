# 14 — Unsupported Claims (Evidence Does NOT Support)

Popular claims that should **not** be presented as science in PranaForge research or marketing. Grade **E** conceptually (claim-level), even when related practices have other legitimate uses.

---

## 1. Law of Attraction / supernatural manifestation

**Claim:** Thoughts/vibrations directly rearrange external reality to deliver desires.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported as a causal physical mechanism.**  
**What can be true instead (psych mechanisms):** Goal priming, selective attention, expectancy, increased effort, self-fulfilling interpersonal behavior—when paired with action.  
**Traditional/spiritual:** May hold personal meaning; that is not scientific validation.  
**Product rule:** Never sell supernatural causation as neuroscience.

---

## 2. “NSDR / Yoga Nidra replaces sleep” (hour-for-hour or physiological equivalence)

**Claim:** A 20-minute NSDR equals a nap/night sleep physiologically.  
**Evidence status:** **Not supported.** Some rest/relaxation and preliminary sleep-quality findings exist; they do **not** show replacement of sleep homeostasis, full synaptic renormalization claims, or equivalent homeostatic sleep functions.  
**Product rule:** Rest-without-sleep framing only.

---

## 3. “Breathwork detoxes toxins / alkaline blood via breathing”

**Claim:** Specific breathing detoxifies organs or durably alkalizes blood for health miracles.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported / pseudoscience** in popular form. Blood pH is tightly regulated; hyperventilation transiently changes CO2/pH and can be dangerous.  
**Related real effect:** Breathing changes CO2 and autonomic state—not “toxin flush” mythology.

---

## 4. “Oxygenate the brain by big mouth breathing / hyperventilation for genius focus”

**Claim:** More oxygen in via forceful breathing acutely boosts intelligence.  
**Evidence status:** **Misleading.** Hyperventilation lowers CO2 and can reduce cerebral blood flow; may cause lightheadedness, not reliable cognitive enhancement.  
**Prefer:** Calm paced breathing or brief activation protocols with safety limits.

---

## 5. “4-7-8 / box breathing is clinically proven to cure anxiety/insomnia”

**Claim:** Exact branded ratios are definitive cures.  
**Evidence status:** **Overstated.** Related slow breathing has supportive evidence; exact branded cures are not established. Insomnia first-line behavioral care is CBT-I.

---

## 6. “Sleep restriction is a harmless biohack—just sleep less in bed”

**Claim:** DIY aggressive sleep restriction is safe for everyone.  
**Evidence status:** **Dangerous oversimplification.** It is a clinical CBT-I component with contraindications (drowsy driving, mania risk, seizures, etc.).

---

## 7. “Alternate nostril balances left/right brain hemispheres”

**Claim:** Nadi shodhana equalizes hemispheric activity in a specific mystical/scientific way.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported as popularly stated.** Hemispheric “balance” claims are typically oversimplified; any benefits more parsimoniously explained by paced breathing + attention.

---

## 8. “Morning sunlight charges mitochondria like a battery via eyes/skin in X minutes guaranteeing optimal hormones”

**Claim:** Over-precise influencer protocols as guaranteed endocrine optimization.  
**Evidence status:** **Overclaimed.** Morning light is well-supported for circadian phase—but viral quantitative guarantees (exact minutes → specific hormone stacks) outrun evidence.

---

## 9. “Visualization alone manifests performance without practice”

**Claim:** Imagery replaces physical practice.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported.** Imagery helps most as adjunct; PETTLEP literature emphasizes combination with practice.

---

## 10. “Gratitude journaling cures depression”

**Claim:** Positive listing replaces treatment.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported as cure.** Small average wellbeing effects in nonclinical/mixed samples; clinical depression needs appropriate care (e.g., BA/CBT/meds as indicated).

---

## 11. “Cold shower every day rewires trauma”

**Claim:** Routine cold exposure is trauma therapy.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported.** Cold face can be a short arousal tool (TIPP); trauma needs appropriate clinical care. Cold also has medical risks.

---

## 12. “Waking at 5am is universally optimal”

**Claim:** One wake time is biologically best for all.  
**Evidence status:** **Unsupported.** Chronotypes differ; consistent schedule + light timing matter more than a moralized clock time.

---

## Language hygiene for founder review

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Proven to cure | Evidence suggests / associated with |
| Replaces sleep | May aid rest/relaxation; not a sleep substitute |
| Manifests reality | Improves planning/motivation when paired with action |
| Detox | Changes arousal/CO2/attention (specific claim) |
| Scientifically balances nadis | Traditional practice; modern evidence limited |
