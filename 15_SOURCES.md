## Per-protocol source pointers

### Cyclic Sighing (Physiological Sigh Pattern)
- Research note: Yilmaz Balban et al., Cell Reports Medicine 2023 (NCT05304000): remote RCT, daily 5-min cyclic sighing vs box breathing, cyclic hyperventilation, mindfulness; cyclic sighing best for positive affect and respiratory-rate reduction. PubMed 36630953; DOI 10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895. Note: HR/HRV did not significantly differ; healthy adults; remote delivery.
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9873947/ ; https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05304000

### Box Breathing (Square Breathing)
- Research note: Comparator arm in Yilmaz Balban et al. 2023 (Cell Rep Med). Broader paced-breathing literature supports slow breathing for HRV/stress more than the specific equal-hold box pattern. Limited high-quality trials isolating box breathing alone.
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/ ; Fincham et al. Sci Rep 2023 https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-27247-y

### Coherent / Resonance Frequency Breathing (~6 breaths/min)
- Research note: Lehrer & Gevirtz Frontiers Psychol 2014 PMC4104929; Steffen et al. 2017 resonance breathing HRV/BP/mood PMC5575449; Frontiers Neurosci 2020 practical guide to RF assessment. Newer work questions necessity of exact individual RF vs fixed 6/min.
- Links: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4104929/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5575449/ ; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2020.570400/full

### Diaphragmatic (Belly) Breathing
- Research note: Diaphragmatic breathing RCTs for anxiety (e.g., Chen et al. related work); Fincham et al. 2023 breathwork meta-analysis (Sci Rep) small–moderate stress effects; technique heterogeneity is a limitation.
- Links: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-27247-y ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27553981/

### 4-7-8 Breathing (as popularized by Weil)
- Research note: Sparse dedicated RCTs for Weil exact protocol; related paced/exhale-focused breathing has better support (Balban 2023; Lehrer HRVB). Small 4-7-8 studies show autonomic/anxiety changes—interpret cautiously.
- Links: Andrew Weil public educational materials (popularization); compare Balban 2023 and Lehrer HRVB literature.

### Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana / Anulom Vilom)
- Research note: Small trials and yoga research reviews report anxiety/HRV changes; quality generally lower than Western paced-breathing RCTs.
- Links: Traditional hatha yoga texts; modern small clinical/yoga studies (heterogeneous).

### Exhale-Emphasized Paced Breathing (No Holds)
- Research note: Aligned with cyclic sighing emphasis (Balban 2023) and slow-breathing literature.
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/

### Cyclic Hyperventilation with Retention (Caution Protocol)
- Research note: Comparator arm in Yilmaz Balban et al. 2023. Distinct from Wim Hof Method (separate evidence/risks).
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/

### Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
- Research note: Manzoni et al. BMC Psychiatry 2008 relaxation meta-analysis PMC2427027; Muhammad Khir et al. 2024 PMR systematic review PMC10844009; AASM 2021 behavioral insomnia guidelines.
- Links: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2427027/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10844009/

### Autogenic Training (AT)
- Research note: Seo & Kim 2019 AT stress meta-analysis PubMed 31477667; earlier AT reviews (Stetter & Kupper).
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31477667/

### Body Scan (MBSR Element)
- Research note: MBSR evidence base (Kabat-Zinn); mindfulness meta-analyses for anxiety/depression. Attribute stronger evidence to MBSR package than stand-alone scan.
- Links: Kabat-Zinn MBSR curriculum; mindfulness clinical trial literature.

### Yoga Nidra / NSDR (Non-Sleep Deep Rest)
- Research note: Datta et al. NMJI 2021 (YN vs CBT-I, small RCT); local sleep EEG PMC9315270; PLOS One 2023 cognition/sleep novices; 2023 sleep-lab feasibility mixed vs quiet rest; EEG systematic reviews heterogeneous.
- Links: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0294678 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9315270/

### Stimulus Control Therapy (CBT-I Component)
- Research note: AASM 2021 CPG for behavioral/psychological insomnia treatments PMC7853203; Edinger et al. systematic review; Furukawa et al. JAMA Psychiatry 2024 component network meta-analysis.
- Links: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7853203/ ; https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2814164

### Sleep Restriction Therapy (CBT-I Component)
- Research note: AASM 2021 guideline; Furukawa et al. JAMA Psychiatry 2024 cNMA—sleep restriction among critical components. ACP CBT-I guideline context.
- Links: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7853203/ ; https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2814164

### Behavioral Wind-Down Routine
- Research note: Sleep hygiene alone insufficient per AASM; wind-down practically useful within multicomponent care.
- Links: AASM behavioral insomnia guidelines.

### Caffeine Timing Cutoff
- Research note: Laboratory caffeine–sleep studies; clinical sleep education summaries. Not a cure for chronic insomnia disorder alone.
- Links: Peer-reviewed caffeine–sleep experimental literature; clinical sleep guidance.

### Morning Outdoor Light Timing
- Research note: Circadian photoreception science; AASM practice parameters for circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders; jet-lag intervention reviews (Frontiers Physiol 2019).
- Links: https://aasm.org/resources/practiceparameters/pp_circadianrhythm.pdf ; https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2019.00927

### Brief Nap Protocol (10–20 min)
- Research note: Nap/performance literature in sleep science; operational studies support brief naps. Insomnia guidelines often discourage naps.
- Links: Sleep research reviews on napping and performance; AASM insomnia guidance on naps.

### Jet Lag Light and Melatonin Timing (Careful Protocol)
- Research note: AASM circadian practice parameters; jet-lag reviews (Frontiers Physiol 2019). Melatonin evidence for jet lag stronger than for primary chronic insomnia.
- Links: https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2019.00927 ; AASM circadian practice parameters

### Fixed Wake-Time Sleep Schedule
- Research note: Circadian science; CBT-I stimulus control includes fixed wake time; social jet lag research.
- Links: CBT-I manuals; circadian medicine reviews.

### Evening Light Hygiene (Dim and Warm)
- Research note: Circadian photoreception and evening light studies; sleep medicine light hygiene guidance.
- Links: Circadian lighting research reviews; AASM-related circadian guidance.

### Acute Physiological Sigh (1–3 Cycles)
- Research note: Balban et al. 2023 primarily studied 5-min daily cyclic sighing; acute 1–3 cycle use is mechanistically related but less directly trial-defined.
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/

### 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding
- Research note: Widely taught in clinical practice. Specific 5-4-3-2-1 RCTs sparse; small student studies exist. Practical coping—not strong experimental grade.
- Links: Clinical psychoeducation (major medical centers); trauma-informed care curricula.

### Brief PMR (Hands-Shoulders-Face)
- Research note: Extrapolated from PMR evidence base (abbreviated PMR commonly used).
- Links: See PMR entry.

### Moderate Aerobic Exercise Bout
- Research note: Cochrane exercise for depression reviews; newer umbrella/meta-analyses of exercise for depression/anxiety. Adjunct, not always monotherapy for major clinical disorders.
- Links: Cochrane Library exercise-depression reviews; recent exercise–anxiety/depression meta-analyses.

### Scheduled Worry / Postpone Worry
- Research note: CBT for GAD literature; Borkovec and related stimulus-control approaches to worry; clinical CBT protocols.
- Links: CBT worry literature; clinical GAD treatment manuals.

### Mindfulness of Breath (Formal Sitting)
- Research note: MBSR evidence base; MBCT for recurrent depression; Balban 2023 used mindfulness as active comparator (breathwork edged mood/RR).
- Links: MBSR/MBCT clinical trial literature; meditation adverse effects reviews.

### Micro Social Connection Check-In
- Research note: Social support and health psychology literature; loneliness intervention research (heterogeneous).
- Links: Health psychology social support reviews.

### Pre-Performance Routine (PPR)
- Research note: Sport psychology reviews on pre-performance routines; experimental studies under pressure. Effects vary by sport/skill and routine quality.
- Links: Applied sport psychology literature on routines under pressure.

### Centering (Ravizza-Style)
- Research note: Ken Ravizza applied sport psychology teachings; empirical support largely from related PPR/breath/attention research.
- Links: Ravizza applied materials; sport psychology centering descriptions.

### PETTLEP Imagery
- Research note: Holmes & Collins 2001 PETTLEP model; 2022 systematic reviews on PETTLEP and sport performance.
- Links: https://doi.org/10.3390/app12199753 ; Holmes & Collins JASP 2001

### Implementation Intentions (If–Then Plans)
- Research note: Gollwitzer & Sheeran meta-analytic work on implementation intentions; extensive experimental literature.
- Links: Gollwitzer & Sheeran implementation intention meta-analyses.

### Tactical Breath Reset (Short Box-like)
- Research note: See box breathing + PPR entries.
- Links: Applied tactical breathing education; Balban 2023 box arm.

### WOOP (Mental Contrasting + Implementation Intentions)
- Research note: Oettingen mental contrasting; WOOP practicalization; Frontiers Psychology 2021 MCII meta-analysis (g≈0.34; trim-and-fill lower). Health behavior MCII meta-analyses similar.
- Links: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.565202/full

### SMART Goals with Evidence Caveats
- Research note: SMART popular management heuristic (Doran 1981 often cited). Goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham) has stronger evidence for specific difficult goals + feedback than for the SMART acronym per se.
- Links: Locke & Latham goal-setting theory reviews; Doran SMART note.

### Habit Stacking (Routine-Based Cueing)
- Research note: Popular science (Atomic Habits). Underlying science: implementation intentions; Wood/Neal habit research; RCTs on routine- vs time-based cue planning.
- Links: Habit formation RCTs; Gollwitzer II literature; Clear as popularizer.

### Process Visualization (vs Outcome-Only Fantasy)
- Research note: Oettingen mental contrasting vs indulging; sport imagery literature; contrast with unsupported Law of Attraction claims.
- Links: Oettingen research program; imagery reviews.

### Values Compass (Brief ACT/Values Clarification)
- Research note: ACT values components; meaning/values psychological research.
- Links: ACT values exercises (Hayes/Wilson); values clarification literature.

### TIPP (DBT Crisis Survival Skill)
- Research note: Linehan DBT manuals; evidence strongest for DBT programs overall, not each micro-skill in isolation. Physiological rationale for cold/dive reflex is plausible.
- Links: DBT skills training handouts (Linehan); clinical DBT outcome literature.

### Opposite Action (DBT Skill — Not Full Therapy)
- Research note: DBT skills manuals; emotion regulation research on behavioral opposite strategies.
- Links: Linehan DBT Emotion Regulation skills.

### Cognitive Reappraisal
- Research note: James Gross emotion regulation framework; CBT outcome literature; experimental reappraisal studies.
- Links: Gross emotion regulation reviews; CBT clinical guidelines.

### Affect Labeling (Putting Feelings into Words)
- Research note: Lieberman et al. 2007 Psychological Science Putting Feelings Into Words (PubMed 17576282); Torre & Lieberman reviews on affect labeling.
- Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17576282/

### Cold Face Stimulation (Dive-Reflex Informed)
- Research note: Dive reflex physiology; DBT TIPP rationale. Clinical skill evidence mostly package-level.
- Links: DBT TIPP; dive reflex physiology references.

### Brief Gratitude Listing
- Research note: Emmons & McCullough gratitude experiments; positive psychology meta-analyses (small effects).
- Links: Gratitude intervention literature (Emmons et al.).

### Self-Distancing Reflection
- Research note: Kross & Ayduk self-distancing research program.
- Links: Kross/Ayduk self-distancing papers.

### Cognitive Defusion (Brief ACT Skill)
- Research note: ACT RCTs/meta-analyses for package; defusion experimental tasks in ACT literature (Hayes et al.).
- Links: Hayes ACT literature; Twohig/Levin ACT reviews.

### Brief Attention Restoration (Soft Fascination Break)
- Research note: Kaplan ART; nature exposure cognitive studies (heterogeneous).
- Links: Kaplan Attention Restoration Theory literature; environmental psychology reviews.

### Task-Switching Buffer
- Research note: Cognitive psychology of task switching; Sophie Leroy attention residue research. Buffer ritual is applied synthesis.
- Links: Task-switching literature; attention residue research.

### Tiny Behavioral Activation Step
- Research note: Behavioral activation RCTs and meta-analyses for depression; BA as efficacious psychotherapy.
- Links: BA clinical trial literature; CBT/BA treatment guidelines.

### Focused Work Block + Break (Pomodoro-style)
- Research note: Limited rigorous trials of classic Pomodoro; related evidence from break/attention and timeboxing practices.
- Links: Francesco Cirillo Pomodoro Technique materials; attention/break research adjacency.

## Research gaps (for founder)

1. More independent replications of cyclic sighing outside Stanford remote trial context.
2. High-quality RCTs isolating box breathing and 4-7-8 vs generic slow breathing.
3. Definitive tests of NSDR/Yoga Nidra vs sleep replacement claims (should remain skeptical).
4. Micro-skill RCTs for TIPP elements and 5-4-3-2-1 specifically.
5. Product-relevant dose-finding (minimum effective minutes) across protocols in the same population.
6. Trauma-sensitive adverse-event reporting for breath holds, body scans, and cold exposure.
7. Clear chronotype-personalization rules for light timing in consumer apps without overclaiming.

## Citation hygiene

- Prefer year + DOI/PubMed in product science footnotes.
- Distinguish **guideline-supported** (CBT-I, light for circadian disorders) from **single-trial promising** (cyclic sighing).
- Do not cite podcasts as primary evidence.
