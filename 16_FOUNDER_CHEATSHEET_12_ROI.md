## The 12

| # | Protocol | Grade | Time | Use when | Why it earns a slot |
|---|----------|-------|------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Morning outdoor light** | A | 5–30 min | Daily AM | Circadian anchor; cheap; compounds sleep + mood |
| 2 | **Stimulus control (CBT-I)** | A | Ongoing | Can’t sleep / bed≠sleep | Best non-drug insomnia lever; staff can cue rules, not invent therapy |
| 3 | **Sleep restriction (CBT-I)** | A | Clinician-guided | Chronic insomnia | Strong but **never DIY-aggressive** in product copy |
| 4 | **Moderate aerobic bout** | A | 20–40 min | Chronic stress / mood load | Highest chronic load reducer in this library |
| 5 | **If–then plans** | A | 2–5 min write | Follow-through fails | Turns intention → cue-bound action |
| 6 | **WOOP** | A | 5–15 min | Goals that stall | Wish + obstacle + plan; beats fantasy visualization |
| 7 | **Cognitive reappraisal** | A | 3–10 min | Stuck negative read | Changes meaning, not just arousal |
| 8 | **Tiny behavioral activation** | A | 2–15 min | Rumination / withdrawal | Motion before motivation |
| 9 | **Cyclic / physiological sigh** | B | 30s–5 min | Acute spike | Fastest studied-family downshift; discreet |
| 10 | **Resonance / coherent breathing (~6/min)** | B | 5–20 min | Daily HRV-style training | Best “breath engine” for repeated practice |
| 11 | **PMR (or brief hands-shoulders-face)** | B | 3–15 min | Somatic brace | Works when breath focus worsens anxiety |
| 12 | **Pre-performance routine + process imagery** | B | 5–15 min | High-stakes moment | Stabilize arousal → attention → first actions |

**Honorable mentions (use often, not in top 12):** caffeine cutoff, evening dim light, fixed wake time, affect labeling, postpone-worry, 10–20 min nap, PETTLEP (deeper imagery), scheduled social micro-check-in.

## One-line “when → which”

- **Heart racing before a call** → physiological sigh (1–3) → affect label → one if–then  
- **Mind won’t stop at 1am** → leave bed if awake ~20 min (stimulus control); PMR in chair; no more in-bed content  
- **Bad night, must perform** → fixed wake + morning light + optional 10–20 nap + short sigh/centering — not “NSDR replaced sleep”  
- **Chronic overload** → exercise + light + sleep rails first; breath/mindfulness as amplifiers  
- **Goal week** → WOOP + 2 if–thens + one tiny BA step  
- **Stage / deal / session** → PPR + process imagery (steps), never outcome-only fantasy
