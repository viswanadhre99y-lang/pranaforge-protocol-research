# Profile — Corporate CEOs / C-Suite

**Client type:** Public/private corporate CEO, CFO, COO, etc.  
**Commercial relevance:** High  
**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 IST


## Evidence grades used
- **A** Strong (guidelines / multiple quality trials)
- **B** Moderate (good studies; limitations)
- **C** Preliminary / mixed / package-level
- **D** Traditional / experiential
- **E** Unsupported popular claim
- **Specificity tags:** (A)=Generic portable · (B)=Profession-specific literature · (C)=Situation-specific adaptation


## 1. Typical day
EA-orchestrated; external meetings; media; board. Personal recovery only if scheduled like a meeting.

## 2. Stress
Reputation, markets, regulators, activist pressure, M&A, geopolitics, ESG optics.

## 3. Cognitive load
High-stakes low-frequency decisions + continuous representation load.

## 4. Physical
Travel, dinners, sedentary; executive health programs (Mayo/Cleveland model) already familiar — position as *between annual exams* operating system.

## 5. Emotional
Constrained emotional expression; isolation at the top.

## 6. Sleep
Jet lag + hospitality alcohol + early calls across zones. CDC: 68% international business travelers report regular negative jet-lag symptoms (Yellow Book 2026 citing survey).

## 7. Travel
Heavy; short trips where **non-adaptation** (stay on home time) is correct per AASM/CDC for <2–3 day trips.

## 8. Pre-event
Earnings, board, government meeting, keynote.

## 9. Post-event
Media aftermath; decision second-guessing.

## 10. Burnout
High-functioning; leave is rare; relapse if calendar unchanged (RTW literature: workplace dialogue matters more than wellness alone — PMC10447379).

## 11. Decision fatigue
Real operationally; lab ego-depletion contested — still useful to schedule irreversible decisions after sleep (practice heuristic C; don’t overclaim A).

## 12. Concentration
Briefing-book overload; need attention restoration microbreaks.

## 13. Arousal
Public-performance sympathetic activation.

## 14. Recovery
Can pay anything; adherence is the scarce resource → staff protocols.

## 15. Spoken
Travel fatigue; sleep; “need an edge”; stress.

## 16. Unspoken
Cognitive decline fear; succession; marital strain; alcohol.

## 17. Pay-for-now
Earnings week; multi-zone week; post-crisis; pre-IPO corp; merger integration.

## 18. Recurring
Jet lag mismanagement; dinner-circuit sleep loss; no protected recovery.

## 19. Why generic yoga fails
Not private enough; not travel-portable; not EA-integrated; spa theater without light/melatonin timing.

## 20. Design
- White-label under concierge/hotel.
- Jet-lag calculator + cue cards.
- Board/earnings stacks.

## White-space
C-suite-specific RCTs rare; combine jet-lag AASM/CDC (A/B) + executive health practice.

## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
