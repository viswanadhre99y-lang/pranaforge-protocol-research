## One-liner
Dense evening social/business calendars where alcohol, late light, and 'on' presence collide with sleep.

## Who
Society hosts, UHNW salon runners, dinner-circuit dealmakers, political/fundraising calendars, wedding-season principals.

## Constraints
- Hard to skip events
- Alcohol/food social pressure
- Late bright venues
- Reputation = visible energy

## Dominant stressors
- Chronic sleep curtailment
- Weekend social jet lag ballooning
- Pre-event anxiety
- Post-event rumination
- Recovery guilt

## Primary jobs-to-be-done
- Show up regulated and warm
- Limit physiological damage from late nights
- Recover next morning without wrecking next night
- Contain social anxiety

## Commercial notes
Pair with household staff + event planners; pre-event regulation + post-event recovery packs.

## Privacy / discretion
Guest lists and venues confidential.

## Lifestyle rails (non-negotiable before exotic tools)
- Alcohol stop ≥3–4h before intended sleep when possible
- One recovery night protected per dense week
- Avoid late caffeine as power-through on event days

## Hard avoids / contraindications (product)
- Sobriety moralizing as only path
- NSDR replacing lost sleep after galas
- Public grounding at tables

## Evidence posture for this type
Alcohol fragments sleep; evening light delays phase; fixed wake + morning light blunt social jet lag; PPR performance psychology moderate evidence.
