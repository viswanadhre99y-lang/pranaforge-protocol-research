> **Disclaimer:** Educational personalization intelligence for PranaForge research — **not medical advice**, not psychotherapy, not a treatment plan. Not PranaForge proprietary session IP. Prefer A/B public protocols as defaults; label C/D honestly; never market E-tier claims as science. **NSDR ≠ sleep replacement.** Melatonin: timed chronobiotic tool — not DIY mega-dosing.

**Slug:** `digital_nomads`  
**Cluster:** D — Travel / Lifestyle / Performance-adjacent  
**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 (Asia/Calcutta)

## One-liner
Location-independent workers with chronic mild jet lag, wifi-driven sleep delay, and weak zeitgeber structure.

## Who
Remote founders, freelancers, creators, crypto-adjacent mobiles on 2–6 week hops or perpetual travel.

## Constraints
- Cheap lodging light pollution
- Coworking late culture
- No staff; self-serve only
- Budget vs UHNW tooling
- Visa/work stress

## Dominant stressors
- Nightlife + async work social jet lag
- Inconsistent wake times across cities
- Evening blue light
- Loneliness–overwork cycles
- Timezone arbitrage destroying sleep

## Primary jobs-to-be-done
- Stabilize circadian despite city hops
- Protect deep work blocks
- Prevent burnout spiral
- Build portable recovery kit

## Commercial notes
Volume/community play; lower ACV; content + self-serve packs; upsell intensives at burnout.

## Privacy / discretion
Lower than UHNW; still respect location OPSEC for some.

## Lifestyle rails (non-negotiable before exotic tools)
- Fixed wake ±30 min even when cities change
- Morning outdoor light non-negotiable
- Caffeine cutoff relative to intended sleep

## Hard avoids / contraindications (product)
- Romanticizing grind-without-sleep
- NSDR replaces sleep
- Universal 5am moralizing (chronotypes differ)
- Unvalidated fasting jet-lag theater

## Evidence posture for this type
Social jet lag (Wittmann/Roenneberg); morning light A-grade; sleep hygiene alone weak monotherapy (AASM) — escalate to stimulus control concepts if insomnia emerges.
