## One-liner
Critical incidents and shiftwork — trauma-informed grounding first; EAP/CISM over cathartic breathwork; circadian sleep anchors.

## Who
Fire, EMS, police, dispatch, SAR — performance/recovery psych with trauma-informed caveats.

## Constraints
- Must remain duty-fit
- Agency EAP/CISM pathways
- Cannot be sedated on shift

## Dominant stressors
- Critical incident stress
- Shiftwork circadian disruption
- Off-duty hypervigilance
- Moral distress
- Sleep fragmentation

## Primary jobs-to-be-done
- En-route channel arousal into task
- Post-call stabilize without flooding
- Night-shift sleep anchor
- Off-duty hypervigilance downshift

## Commercial notes
Low–Med luxury concierge; stronger occupational/EAP channel. Station reset-room whitespace.

## Privacy / discretion
Incidents and personal crises confidential; HIPAA/agency rules.

## Lifestyle rails (non-negotiable before exotic tools)
- Protect longest dark sleep opportunity post-shift
- Caffeine early in shift not late
- Peer support / EAP visible
- Fitness-for-duty policies supersede preference

## Hard avoids / contraindications (product)
- DIY catharsis hyperventilation post-call
- Intense body-scan if dissociating/flooding
- Replacing CISM/EAP with wellness workshop
- Breath holds on duty
- Substance as primary post-call downshift

## Evidence posture for this type
