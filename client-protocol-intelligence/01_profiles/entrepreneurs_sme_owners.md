# Profile — Entrepreneurs / SME Owners

**Client type:** SME owners, India family-business operators, bootstrapped entrepreneurs  
**Commercial relevance:** High (India density)  
**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 IST


## Evidence grades used
- **A** Strong (guidelines / multiple quality trials)
- **B** Moderate (good studies; limitations)
- **C** Preliminary / mixed / package-level
- **D** Traditional / experiential
- **E** Unsupported popular claim
- **Specificity tags:** (A)=Generic portable · (B)=Profession-specific literature · (C)=Situation-specific adaptation


## 1. Typical day
Shop floor / WhatsApp vendor calls / bank / family lunch with business talk. Boundaries between home and firm porous — especially joint families.

## 2. Stress sources
Cashflow, receivables, labor, regulation/tax inspections, family shareholders, community status.

## 3. Cognitive load
Practical problem-solving under money threat; less “abstract strategy,” more chronic vigilance.

## 4. Physical
Long sitting or standing; irregular meals; diabetes/metabolic risk common in India UHNW-adjacent — **screen medically, don’t protocol disease**.

## 5. Emotional
Pride + shame oscillations; family criticism; anger at staff; festival-season exhaustion.

## 6. Sleep
Late social/business dinners; early temple/market wake; rumination about payments.

## 7. Travel
Domestic travel heavy; some China/Dubai sourcing; less glamorous jet lag but real fatigue.

## 8. Pre-event
Bank meetings, big buyer negotiations, family governance meetings.

## 9. Post-event
Relief drinking; delayed crash.

## 10. Burnout
“Owner’s curse” — cannot leave; 3Rs (Respite, Reappraisal, Regimen) highly relevant (Williamson et al. 2021).

## 11. Decision fatigue
Every rupee decision lands on owner; needs delegation scripts + if–then.

## 12. Concentration
Constant interruption culture; soft-fascination breaks (Attention Restoration — C) underused.

## 13. Arousal
Anger/irritability more than “anxiety” label; PMR may beat breath for some (B).

## 14. Recovery
Religious ritual may already exist — respect; add evidence rails without erasing meaning.

## 15. Spoken
“Tension.” “BP.” “No time for gym.” (Health anxiety language common)

## 16. Unspoken
Fear of business failure as family failure; succession dread.

## 17. Pay-for-now
Before big tender; after tax notice; wedding-season for business family; liquidity crunch week.

## 18. Recurring
Sleep + metabolic neglect; anger dysregulation; zero detachment.

## 19. Why generic yoga fails
Often already “do yoga” socially without load management; needs business-timed microprotocols in Hindi/English mix culturally; massage without cashflow stress skills fails.

## 20. Design
- Frame as *clarity for negotiations* and *sleep for judgment*.
- Family-office adjacent packaging for wealthier SME.
- Avoid shaming traditional practices; add mechanism labels.

## White-space
India SME occupational recovery trials scarce in Western databases — local partner observation = research method.

## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
