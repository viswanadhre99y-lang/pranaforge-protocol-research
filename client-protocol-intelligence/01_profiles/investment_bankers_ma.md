## 1. Typical day (live deal)
Model/deck iterations; client emails at all hours; staffer → VP → MD review loops. Juniors: 70–95h weeks documented in industry surveys (WSO 2021–22; Goldman “13” ~95h / ~5h sleep — Reuters/BBC 2021).

## 2. Stress
Deadlines, perfectionism culture, status, client unpredictability, sleep deprivation as norm.

## 3. Cognitive load
High detail accuracy under fatigue; error risk rises with sleep loss.

## 4. Physical

## 5. Emotional

## 6. Sleep
Core pathology — often ≤5–6h on deals. Not fixable by breath alone.

## 7. Travel

## 14. Recovery

## 15. Spoken

## 17. Pay-for-now
Between deals; post-close week; roadshow; MD client-dinner circuit.

## 18. Recurring
Sleep debt; alcohol downshift; no aerobic base.

## 19. Why generic yoga fails
Literally no time in live deal; culture mocks wellness; need 90-second resets + post-deal packages + sleep opportunity advocacy (org), not studio membership.

## 20. Design
- **Only sell seriously to MD+ / post-deal windows** in Phase-1.
- Micro-resets for live deal; full stack only between deals.
- Never imply protocols make 95h weeks safe.

## White-space
Occupational surveys strong on problem; intervention RCTs in IB nearly nonexistent — white-space with ethics constraints.

## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
