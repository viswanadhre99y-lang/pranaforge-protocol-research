## One-liner
Contracted pros under fixture, media, and travel pressure; need composure tools that survive team medical gatekeeping.

## Who
Paid professional athletes across individual and team sports (non-Olympic-primary); UHNW subset with private travel and suite recovery.

## Constraints
- Team medical / S&C approval for novel protocols
- Anti-doping optics — clean wellness language only
- Micro-windows between warm-up and start
- Locker / shared-space privacy limits

## Dominant stressors
- Choking / evaluation under pressure
- Fixture congestion + travel fatigue
- Injury return anxiety and identity threat
- Late games / time-zone sleep disruption
- Media scrutiny and social comparison

## Primary jobs-to-be-done
- Stabilize pre-compete arousal and attention
- Recover between fixtures without residual rumination
- Protect sleep on travel weeks
- Support return-to-play mental reps without replacing rehab

## Commercial notes
Med–High via sports physio/teams and UHNW athlete concierge overlays; cricket/golf/tennis India-adjacent density.

## Privacy / discretion
Locker-room discreet; suite/jet for UHNW; never leak injury or selection narratives.

## Lifestyle rails (non-negotiable before exotic tools)
- Sleep opportunity + caffeine timing vs compete clock
- Morning outdoor light when schedule allows
- Team medical clearance for heat/cold
- Hydration / fueling per S&C — not wellness substitution

## Hard avoids / contraindications (product)
- Hyperventilation + ice + breath-hold stacks
- Breath practice in water or while driving
- Body-shame framing in ED-risk sports
- NSDR as sleep replacement after red-eyes
- Outcome-only manifestation as performance method (E)
- DIY aggressive CBT-I sleep restriction
