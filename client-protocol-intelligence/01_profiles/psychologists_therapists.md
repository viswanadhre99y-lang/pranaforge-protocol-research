## Who
Clinical/counseling psychologists, psychotherapists, counselors; private practice and agency.

## Dominant stressors
- Empathy load / compassion fatigue
- Secondary traumatic stress
- Caseload acuity mix
- Boundary erosion with clients
- Isolation in private practice
- Burnout + STS overlap

## Constraints (protocol design)
- Session back-to-backs
- Confidentiality limits peer venting
- Ethical dual-role vigilance

## Safety / contraindications notes
Self-care protocols are adjuncts — not treatment for clinician impairment. Trauma processing of own material needs personal therapy.

## High-value moments
- Between heavy sessions
- After trauma narrative exposure
- End-of-day caseload residue
- Supervision gaps
- Client suicide-risk weeks

## Literature notes (population-specific)
Reviews: mindfulness/ACT/self-compassion/supervision promising; burnout effects inconsistent; org factors essential. Vicarious trauma intervention evidence limited/heterogeneous. Compassion-fatigue meta-analysis heterogeneous.

## Distinctive finding
Therapists need **between-session residue management + supervision boundaries** more than more meditation apps. Evidence for self-care is promising but mixed; white-space: brief between-session RCTs.
