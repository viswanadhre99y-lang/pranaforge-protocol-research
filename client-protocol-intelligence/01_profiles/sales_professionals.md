## Safety / contraindications notes

## High-value moments

- Pre-discovery call

- End-of-month quota panic
- Post-win comedown

## Literature notes (population-specific)
Turner et al. 2024: 4-week online REBT for UK sales pros reduced irrational beliefs and negative-emotion persistence. Sales resilience linked to calling effort after setbacks (correlational). Digital resilience training RCTs in general workers.

## Distinctive finding
Sales: **rejection recovery + irrational target beliefs** are the distinctive layer (REBT trial). Pair with BA to keep pipeline behavior moving. White-space: larger preregistered sales RCTs.
