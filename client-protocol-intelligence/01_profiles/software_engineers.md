## Who
IC engineers, tech leads, SREs; remote/hybrid and on-site.

## Dominant stressors
- Always-on Slack/PagerDuty
- Context switching
- Deploy anxiety
- Blurred work–non-work boundaries
- Sitting/MSD
- Imposter in review culture

## Constraints (protocol design)
- Deep work needs vs meetings
- On-call rotations
- Screen-heavy evenings

## Safety / contraindications notes
DIY aggressive sleep restriction unsafe with on-call driving/ops. Hyperventilation near water never.

## High-value moments
- Pre-deploy
- Post-incident
- On-call night
- PR review rejection
- Deep work protect

## Literature notes (population-specific)
Online recovery training with detachment + iCBT-I elements improved insomnia in blurred-boundary workers (RCT). Workplace insomnia interventions (mostly CBT-I) moderate effects. Insomnia reduction can mediate stress/exhaustion improvements.

## Distinctive finding
Highest-ROI profession layer is **boundary/detachment + insomnia rails** (iCBT-I–informed recovery training evidence). White-space: engineer-only RCTs still limited; borrow blurred-boundary worker trials.
