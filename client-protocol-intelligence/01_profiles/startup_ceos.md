# Profile — Startup CEOs (Scaled)

**Client type:** Startup CEOs post-PMF / larger org  
**Commercial relevance:** High  
**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 IST


## Evidence grades used
- **A** Strong (guidelines / multiple quality trials)
- **B** Moderate (good studies; limitations)
- **C** Preliminary / mixed / package-level
- **D** Traditional / experiential
- **E** Unsupported popular claim
- **Specificity tags:** (A)=Generic portable · (B)=Profession-specific literature · (C)=Situation-specific adaptation


## 1. Typical day
Calendar-owned: 1:1s, leadership meetings, board prep, external brand. Deep work scarce unless protected. Decisions are people-and-governance heavy vs early founder product tinkering.

## 2. Primary stress sources
Role loneliness; board expectations; key talent retention; scaling culture; public narrative; still residual runway/market risk.

## 3. Cognitive load
Meta-cognition about org (strategy) while firefighting. High context switching across altitudes (vision ↔ escalation).

## 4. Physical fatigue
Travel + dinner circuit; underused body; tension headaches; “Sunday scaries” somatic.

## 5. Emotional fatigue
Holding others’ anxiety; few peers; guilt when resting; anger spikes then over-control.

## 6. Sleep
Better resources than early founders but late dinners, alcohol at events, early flights. Sleep quality > quantity problem common.

## 7. Travel
Board offsites, customers, conferences (Web Summit–class); east/west jet lag for global SaaS.

## 8. Pre-event
Board meetings, all-hands, keynotes — need centering + process PPR (Ravizza-style — C; sports psych analog).

## 9. Post-event
After board: rumination and “should have said.” Need cognitive defusion / postpone-worry (C/B) + wind-down.

## 10. Burnout
Executive burnout with high-functioning mask; relapse when returning to same calendar (SimplePractice executive burnout practice literature — clinical framing; wellness layer only for us).

## 11. Decision fatigue
Executive suite decision volume; needs “decision diet” (batch, delegate, peak-hour protection) — practice-based, Evidence C for scheduling heuristics; ego-depletion literature mixed (note honestly).

## 12. Concentration
Interrupted by Slack/EA; needs protected strategic blocks (TallRock / FO self-care essays echo this — practice D/C).

## 13. Arousal
Pre-all-hands spike; post-conflict residue with reports.

## 14. Recovery capacity
Can buy massage/spa but won’t protect sleep or calendar; staff can cue if given scripts.

## 15. Spoken
“No time.” “Need energy for board.” “Travel wrecks me.”

## 16. Unspoken
Loneliness; fear of being replaced; marriage strain; substance soft misuse (alcohol).

## 17. Pay-for-now
Board week; keynote; post-layoff week; fundraising close; post-acquisition integration.

## 18. Recurring
Calendar obesity; travel debt; alcohol-as-downshift; no peer recovery community.

## 19. Why generic yoga fails
Doesn’t survive calendar; not integrated with EA; “spa day” without circadian plan fails next Monday.

## 20. Design implications
- EA/CoS cue cards are the product surface.
- Board-week and travel-arc packages.
- Emphasize sustainable leadership capacity, not chill.

## White-space
Thin CEO-specific breath/recovery RCTs; borrow executive health + sports psych + jet-lag science.

## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
