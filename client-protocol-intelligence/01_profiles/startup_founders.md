# Profile — Startup Founders

**Client type:** Startup founders (pre-scale emphasis; India + global analogs)  
**Commercial relevance:** High (Phase-1)  
**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 IST  
**Sources:** Entrepreneurial sleep/recovery literature (Williamson, Gish, Stephan et al.); sleep deprivation cognition reviews; occupational burnout RTW literature (context).


## Evidence grades used
- **A** Strong (guidelines / multiple quality trials)
- **B** Moderate (good studies; limitations)
- **C** Preliminary / mixed / package-level
- **D** Traditional / experiential
- **E** Unsupported popular claim
- **Specificity tags:** (A)=Generic portable · (B)=Profession-specific literature · (C)=Situation-specific adaptation


## 1. Typical day
Wake often already in Slack/WhatsApp. Deep work fragmented by fundraising, hiring, customer fires. Meals irregular. Evening “catch-up” coding/metrics into night. Identity: *I am the company.*

## 2. Primary stress sources
Runway/cash; key-person dependency; rejection (investors/customers); co-founder conflict; family expectations (India: “stable job” narrative). Chronic uncertainty more than single acute events.

## 3. Cognitive load pattern
High working-memory switching (product ↔ people ↔ cash). Sleep loss impairs opportunity evaluation and belief formation about venture ideas (Gish/Wagner et al. line of work; JBV sleep & ideation). Perseveration and risky decisions rise with deprivation (Harrison & Horne; Killgore).

## 4. Physical fatigue signature
Neck/shoulder brace from laptop; under-recovered training or zero training; caffeine-masked fatigue; GI irregularity from meal skipping.

## 5. Emotional fatigue signature
Founder loneliness; shame after “failed” pitches; irritability with team; emotional suppression (“must look confident”).

## 6. Sleep pattern
Voluntary short sleep as badge; irregular bedtime; weekend “catch-up” that fails to fully restore vigilance after chronic restriction (operational sleep science). Insomnia when rumination about runway hits bed.

## 7. Travel pattern
Pitch travel, customer visits, accelerator programs; often short-haul red-eyes; poor pre-adaptation.

## 8. Pre-event needs (pitch, demo, board)
Downshift arousal without sedation; sharpen attention; process imagery of *first 60 seconds*, not fantasy outcome (PETTLEP/process viz — Evidence B; outcome-only fantasy weaker).

## 9. Post-event needs
Discharge sympathetic activation; prevent doomscrolling metrics; sleep opportunity protection.

## 10. Burnout pattern
Entrepreneurial exhaustion; detachment failure; “hustle relapse.” Recovery interventions framed as Respite / Reappraisal / Regimen (Williamson, Gish & Stephan, *Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice*, 2021 — conceptual/profession-specific B-frame; RCTs still thin → white-space).

## 11. Decision fatigue pattern
Too many micro-decisions (tools, hires, pricing). Needs batching + if–then rules (Implementation Intentions — Evidence A generic).

## 12. Concentration pattern
Context-switch tax; “fake deep work” with notifications on. Needs task-switching buffers and focused blocks (Evidence C generic).

## 13. Arousal regulation
Oscillates hypoarousal (crash) and hyperarousal (launch week). Physiological sigh for spikes (Balban et al. 2023 — B); resonance breathing for daily load (Lehrer — B).

## 14. Recovery capacity
Low baseline; identity blocks rest (“if I rest, company dies”). Must reframe recovery as fiduciary duty to venture judgment quality.

## 15. Spoken problems (what they’ll say)
“I just need more hours.” “Sleep when we raise.” “Anxious before pitches.” “Can’t switch off.”

## 16. Unspoken problems
Fear of looking weak to co-founders/investors; relationship decay; imposter syndrome; grief if prior failure.

## 17. Pay-for-now moments
Pre-pitch week; post-rejection night; fundraising roadshow; launch week crash; first hire failure aftermath.

## 18. Recurring problems
Chronic sleep debt; decision clutter; inability to detach; caffeine dependence; weekend non-recovery.

## 19. Why generic yoga/massage fails
Too long; studio logistics; “wellness” framing clashes with founder identity; no tie to pitch/runway moments; massage without sleep/circadian rails doesn’t restore judgment.

## 20. Design implications for PranaForge
- 3–12 minute protocols only; phone-down / staff-cueable.
- Tie every protocol to a founder moment (pitch, reject, board).
- Lead with *decision quality / judgment*, not “relax.”
- Pair sleep rails (light, caffeine cutoff, stimulus control cues) before exotic breath.
- Never claim to treat anxiety disorders.

## White-space
Few RCTs of recovery protocols **in founders specifically** (mostly sleep correlational + conceptual 3Rs). Profession-specific packaging of generic A/B tools is the product moat.

## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
