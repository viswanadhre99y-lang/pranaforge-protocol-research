## Safety & boundaries (all Cluster A)
- **Wellness ≠ medical treatment.** Persistent insomnia, depression, panic, chest pain, syncope, or substance dependence → licensed clinician.
- **Breath-hold / hyperventilation:** avoid if pregnancy, uncontrolled hypertension, epilepsy, severe respiratory disease, or panic with breath-focus intolerance; never near water.
- **Cold/heat:** medical clearance if CVD; no extreme cold after alcohol.
- **Sleep restriction (CBT-I component):** clinician-guided only — never DIY-aggressive.
- **Melatonin / hypnotics / stimulants:** discuss with physician; CDC notes 0.5–1 mg often sufficient for circadian shift; wrong timing worsens jet lag; >5 mg not recommended for shifting (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
- **Manifestation:** separate (1) psych mechanisms (implementation intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP — evidence-backed) from (2) spiritual practice (personal) from (3) unsupported metaphysical claims (Law of Attraction as causality — Evidence E).
