### Owner Detachment Alarm (WhatsApp Boundary If–Then)

- **Protocol:** Owner Detachment Alarm (WhatsApp Boundary If–Then)
- **Client type:** SME owners
- **Specific situation:** Always-on vendor/staff chat
- **Problem:** No psychological detachment; evening anger spikes
- **When:** Evenings + weekly rest day
- **Duration:** Ongoing rule
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Instructions:** If–then: If message after 21:00 and not safety/cash-critical, then morning template reply. Whitelist emergencies. Announce to key staff. Pair with 10-min phone-away walk. India: 3 dinners/week without ops talk.
- **Immediate effect:** Fewer evening spikes
- **Longer-term:** Better sleep opportunity; steadier next-day negotiation
- **Mechanism:** Psychological detachment / recovery; implementation intentions
- **Evidence:** B/C — detachment lit + II (A); SME packaging C
- **Population studied:** Occupational recovery research; entrepreneurs 3Rs framework
- **Limitations:** Real emergencies need whitelist
- **Contraindications:** None
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Family buy-in helps
- **Source:** Sonnentag detachment; Williamson 3Rs; Gollwitzer II
- **Creator:** Situation adaptation
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Pre-Negotiation Calm-Anger (PMR + Reappraisal + Exhale Pace)

- **Protocol:** Pre-Negotiation Calm-Anger (PMR + Reappraisal + Exhale Pace)
- **Client type:** SME owners
- **Specific situation:** Bank/buyer/family shareholder meeting
- **Problem:** Irritability harming deal terms
- **When:** T-15 min
- **Duration:** 8–12 min
- **Frequency:** Per meeting
- **Instructions:** Brief PMR jaw-shoulders-hands → reappraisal cue (“information gathering, not attack”) → 2 min exhale-emphasized breathing without holds → one aim sentence.
- **Immediate effect:** Lower hostility; clearer voice
- **Longer-term:** Reputation as steady negotiator
- **Mechanism:** Somatic release; cognitive reappraisal; paced breathing
- **Evidence:** A/B components; situation C
- **Population studied:** General anxiety/anger regulation; not SME RCT
- **Limitations:** Won’t fix bad unit economics
- **Contraindications:** Uncontrolled HTN — gentle PMR only
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** No
- **Source:** Reappraisal literature; PMR meta-analyses; slow breathing reviews
- **Creator:** Composite
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### SME 3Rs Sunday Regimen Block

- **Protocol:** SME 3Rs Sunday Regimen Block
- **Client type:** SME / entrepreneurs
- **Specific situation:** Weekly reset in family-business context
- **Problem:** No regimen; festival/work blur
- **When:** Sunday or local rest day
- **Duration:** 90–150 min protected
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Instructions:** Respite: non-business social or nature/temple without calls. Reappraisal: one page — worry that wasn’t true. Regimen: regular meals; 20–40 min walk/aerobic; bedtime plan for week.
- **Immediate effect:** Subjective restoration
- **Longer-term:** Lower exhaustion risk (framework-level)
- **Mechanism:** 3Rs entrepreneurial recovery
- **Evidence:** B conceptual + A exercise
- **Population studied:** Entrepreneurs framework (Williamson et al. 2021)
- **Limitations:** Family resistance
- **Contraindications:** Cardiac symptoms → physician before new exercise
- **Safety:** Medical clearance if indicated
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Optional
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/10422587211006431
- **Creator:** Williamson et al. framework applied
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Receivables Rumination Postpone + Stimulus Control

- **Protocol:** Receivables Rumination Postpone + Stimulus Control
- **Client type:** SME owners
- **Specific situation:** Night worry about payments
- **Problem:** Insomnia from cash rumination
- **When:** Bedtime window
- **Duration:** 10 min slot + overnight rules
- **Frequency:** Nightly when active
- **Instructions:** Early evening 10-min money-worry slot with list + next actions. After slot, postpone. Bed=sleep. If awake, leave bed. Morning light.
- **Immediate effect:** Less in-bed arousal
- **Longer-term:** Improved sleep efficiency
- **Mechanism:** CBT worry postpone + stimulus control
- **Evidence:** A/B
- **Population studied:** Insomnia populations
- **Limitations:** Real cash crisis needs business action too
- **Contraindications:** Chronic insomnia → full CBT-I
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Clinician if chronic
- **Source:** CBT-I components (AASM)
- **Creator:** Public CBT
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
