### Residence Sleep Standard Card (Staff-Operable)

- **Protocol:** Residence Sleep Standard Card (Staff-Operable)
- **Client type:** FO / UHNW
- **Specific situation:** Multiple homes / hotels / yachts
- **Problem:** Environment variance destroys sleep opportunity
- **When:** Every sleep location
- **Duration:** Setup 10–20 min; nightly
- **Frequency:** Continuous
- **Instructions:** Staff checklist: cool dark quiet; same wind-down window; caffeine cutoff; morning light exposure plan per timezone; no devices in bed; optional PMR audio 10 min. Principal does not invent — staff executes.
- **Immediate effect:** Higher sleep opportunity consistency
- **Longer-term:** Cross-residence circadian stability
- **Mechanism:** Stimulus control + hygiene + circadian light
- **Evidence:** A/B components; FO delivery model C
- **Population studied:** Insomnia hygiene/stimulus control; not UHNW RCT
- **Limitations:** Staff training required
- **Contraindications:** Clinical insomnia → CBT-I clinician
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Staff-led
- **Supervision?:** Lifestyle manager
- **Source:** CBT-I stimulus control; circadian light
- **Creator:** Ops design for FO

### Family Council Pre-Regulation Stack

- **Protocol:** Family Council Pre-Regulation Stack
- **Client type:** FO principals / family
- **Specific situation:** Family governance / succession meeting
- **Problem:** Identity threat + anger → destructive conflict
- **When:** T-20 min and during breaks
- **Duration:** 10–15 min pre; 2 min breaks
- **Frequency:** Per council
- **Instructions:** Pre: PMR jaw/hands + reappraisal (“roles vs persons”) + values sentence + 3 sighs. Break: leave room, water, no ally-texting escalation. If–then: “If I feel attacked, then ask one clarifying question before responding.”
- **Immediate effect:** Lower explosive turns
- **Longer-term:** Better governance climate
- **Mechanism:** Emotion regulation; II
- **Evidence:** B/C
- **Population studied:** Family therapy/emotion regulation general; FO practice
- **Limitations:** Deep conflicts need mediators
- **Contraindications:** Domestic violence → specialist
- **Safety:** Escalate appropriately
- **Self-admin?:** With facilitator ideally
- **Supervision?:** Family facilitator/coach
- **Source:** Reappraisal; II; PMR
- **Creator:** FO situation package

### Private Aviation Micro Jet-Lag Protocol

- **Protocol:** Private Aviation Micro Jet-Lag Protocol
- **Client type:** UHNW / FO principals
- **Specific situation:** PJ hops 2–6 zones, short stays
- **Problem:** Micro misalignment + dinner circuit
- **When:** Each hop
- **Duration:** Trip plan
- **Frequency:** Per travel
- **Instructions:** Decide adapt vs home-time (short stay → home-time). Light SEEK/AVOID per direction. Melatonin only if timed (0.5–1 mg). Alcohol cap on flight. Staff sets cabin light cues when possible. Arrival: short nap rules; morning light at destination if adapting.
- **Immediate effect:** Better first meeting day
- **Longer-term:** Less cumulative PJ fatigue
- **Mechanism:** Circadian
- **Evidence:** A/B CDC
- **Population studied:** Travelers; business jet users understudied specifically
- **Limitations:** Social schedule overrides
- **Contraindications:** Melatonin with physician
- **Safety:** As jet lag
- **Self-admin?:** Staff-assisted
- **Supervision?:** Optional travel med
- **Source:** CDC Yellow Book 2026
- **Creator:** CDC-aligned FO packaging
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Principal–Staff Cue Drill (2-Minute Downshift on Request)

- **Protocol:** Principal–Staff Cue Drill (2-Minute Downshift on Request)
- **Client type:** FO ecosystem
- **Specific situation:** Principal escalating mid-day
- **Problem:** No portable reset that staff can offer without awkwardness
- **When:** On cue word
- **Duration:** 2 min
- **Frequency:** As needed
- **Instructions:** Agreed cue (“reset”). Staff: water + leave room 2 min. Principal: 3 sighs OR cold face water 20–30s (dive-reflex informed, C) + one affect label. Resume.
- **Immediate effect:** Fast partial downshift
- **Longer-term:** Normalized recovery culture without spa theater
- **Mechanism:** Breath / dive reflex / affect label
- **Evidence:** B/C components
- **Population studied:** Breath/cold face literature limited; practice C
- **Limitations:** Cold face: CVD caution
- **Contraindications:** Cold water face if cardiac issues — skip
- **Safety:** No ice-water breath-hold; no water immersion
- **Self-admin?:** Staff-cued
- **Supervision?:** Lifestyle manager trains
- **Source:** Balban; dive reflex cold-face literature (preliminary)
- **Creator:** FO ops design
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
