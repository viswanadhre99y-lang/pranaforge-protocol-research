### Live-Deal 90-Second Reset (Non-Negotiable Micro)

- **Protocol:** Live-Deal 90-Second Reset (Non-Negotiable Micro)
- **Client type:** IB analysts→VPs on live deal
- **Specific situation:** Between deck iterations at 1am
- **Problem:** Sympathetic thrash + error risk under sleep debt
- **When:** Every 60–90 min of continuous work
- **Duration:** 90 seconds
- **Frequency:** Hourly on live deal
- **Instructions:** Stand; 3 physiological sighs; eyes to far point 20s; water; one stretch. No phone dopamine. Does NOT replace sleep.
- **Immediate effect:** Tiny clarity bump
- **Longer-term:** Slightly fewer crash errors (plausible, unproven in IB RCT)
- **Mechanism:** Brief autonomic + attentional reset
- **Evidence:** C — sigh B; package C; IB intervention RCT absent
- **Population studied:** Healthy adults sigh; IB only survey problem data (WSO; Goldman 13 Reuters/BBC 2021)
- **Limitations:** Cannot neutralize 95h weeks
- **Contraindications:** Dizziness — sit
- **Safety:** Explicit: not safety clearance for sleep-deprived work
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Team culture
- **Source:** Balban 2023; WSO/Reuters IB hours context
- **Creator:** Harm-reduction micro
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Post-Close 72-Hour Recovery Arc

- **Protocol:** Post-Close 72-Hour Recovery Arc
- **Client type:** IB (esp. MD/ED commercial ICP)
- **Specific situation:** After deal announcement/close
- **Problem:** Crash, alcohol binge, sleep rebound chaos
- **When:** 72h post-close
- **Duration:** 3-day arc
- **Frequency:** Per close
- **Instructions:** Day0 evening: alcohol cap or none; wind-down PMR. Day1: fixed wake not ultra-late; outdoor light; easy aerobic 20–40 min; short nap rules. Day2: resume caffeine discipline; one social non-work contact; postpone next staffing if possible. Screen for depression if anhedonia persists.
- **Immediate effect:** Softer landing
- **Longer-term:** Less cumulative damage across years
- **Mechanism:** Sleep rebound management; BA; alcohol avoidance
- **Evidence:** C package; components A/B
- **Population studied:** General recovery; IB surveys document problem not solution RCTs
- **Limitations:** Staffing culture may deny Days
- **Contraindications:** Substance dependence → clinical
- **Safety:** Escalate mental health
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Coach for MD+
- **Source:** Sleep rebound literature; BA; IB conditions surveys
- **Creator:** Situation package
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
