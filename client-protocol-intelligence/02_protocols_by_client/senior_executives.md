### QBR / Promo Pre-Meeting Centering

- **Protocol:** QBR / Promo Pre-Meeting Centering
- **Client type:** Senior executives
- **Specific situation:** Quarterly business review or promotion panel
- **Problem:** Evaluative anxiety
- **When:** T-10 min
- **Duration:** 5–8 min
- **Frequency:** Per event
- **Instructions:** Recognize nerves → release via 3 sighs or box 4×4 without strain → refocus on 3 facts to open with. Process imagery first answer.
- **Immediate effect:** Steadier voice
- **Longer-term:** Portable meeting PPR
- **Mechanism:** Centering PPR
- **Evidence:** C
- **Population studied:** Sports centering analogs
- **Limitations:** Won’t fix weak metrics
- **Contraindications:** Breath-hold intolerance — use sighs only
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** No
- **Source:** Ravizza centering tradition; Balban
- **Creator:** Adaptation
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Global-TZ Mild Restriction Survival (Fixed Wake + Light + Nap Rules)

- **Protocol:** Global-TZ Mild Restriction Survival (Fixed Wake + Light + Nap Rules)
- **Client type:** Senior executives on US/EU/India bridges
- **Specific situation:** Chronic early/late calls
- **Problem:** Mild chronic sleep restriction
- **When:** Ongoing
- **Duration:** Lifestyle rails
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Instructions:** Protect one fixed wake; morning light; caffeine cutoff; prefer shifting late meetings over fragmenting sleep when power allows; 10–20 min early nap if needed; never stack alcohol + late call.
- **Immediate effect:** Less fog
- **Longer-term:** Slower accumulation of debt
- **Mechanism:** Sleep homeostasis + circadian
- **Evidence:** A/B sleep restriction science (chronic restriction impairs vigilance — Van Dongen line)
- **Population studied:** Lab sleep restriction; operational settings
- **Limitations:** Org culture may block
- **Contraindications:** None
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Manager negotiation
- **Source:** Sleep restriction performance literature; caffeine timing
- **Creator:** Ops rails
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Meeting-Obesity Micro-Break Protocol

- **Protocol:** Meeting-Obesity Micro-Break Protocol
- **Client type:** Senior executives
- **Specific situation:** Back-to-back calendar
- **Problem:** Attention residue / irritability
- **When:** Between meetings
- **Duration:** 2–5 min
- **Frequency:** Hourly when stacked
- **Instructions:** Stand; 60s soft-fascination gaze out window OR 3 sighs; one sentence intention for next meeting; water. No email rabbit hole.
- **Immediate effect:** Reset attention
- **Longer-term:** Lower end-of-day cognitive sludge
- **Mechanism:** Attention residue reduction; ART microdose
- **Evidence:** C
- **Population studied:** Task-switching / ART adjacent
- **Limitations:** Culture of back-to-backs
- **Contraindications:** None
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** No

### Burnout Early-Warning Behavioral Activation Nudge

- **Protocol:** Burnout Early-Warning Behavioral Activation Nudge
- **Client type:** Senior executives
- **Specific situation:** Emerging exhaustion / cynicism
- **Problem:** Withdrawal feeding burnout
- **When:** When noticing numbness/irritability weeks
- **Duration:** 10–20 min actions
- **Frequency:** 3–5×/week
- **Instructions:** Tiny BA: short walk with colleague (not status meeting), or 20 min aerobic. Values check: what still matters. If exhaustion severe / sick leave — org RTW process + clinician; wellness alone insufficient (RTW burnout reviews).
- **Immediate effect:** Mood lift small
- **Longer-term:** May slow spiral if early
- **Mechanism:** BA; JD-R awareness
- **Evidence:** A for BA; burnout RTW evidence emphasizes workplace dialogue (PMC10447379) — package C
- **Population studied:** Depression BA; burnout RTW employees
- **Limitations:** Not treatment for clinical burnout/depression
- **Contraindications:** Severe symptoms → clinician / OH
- **Safety:** Escalate
- **Self-admin?:** Early only
- **Supervision?:** OH/clinician if moderate-severe
- **Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10447379/ ; BA literature
- **Creator:** Composite with RTW caveat
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
