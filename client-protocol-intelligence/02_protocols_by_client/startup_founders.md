### Founder Sleep Opportunity Guard (Fixed Wake + Light + Caffeine Cutoff + Stimulus-Control Cue)

- **Protocol:** Founder Sleep Opportunity Guard (Fixed Wake + Light + Caffeine Cutoff + Stimulus-Control Cue)
- **Client type:** Startup founders
- **Specific situation:** Chronic voluntary sleep sacrifice during build/fundraise
- **Problem:** Sleep debt impairing opportunity evaluation and emotional regulation
- **When:** Nightly rails; after bad nights
- **Duration:** Ongoing rules; wind-down 20–40 min
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Instructions:** 1) Fixed wake ±30 min. 2) Morning outdoor light 5–30 min. 3) Caffeine cutoff ~8–10h before target sleep. 4) If awake in bed ~20 min, leave bed for low-light non-screen activity. 5) No fundraising email in bed. 6) Optional 10–20 min early nap only if not insomnia-prone.
- **Immediate effect:** Better next-day vigilance vs chaotic sleep
- **Longer-term:** Circadian anchor; less conditioned bed-arousal
- **Mechanism:** Photic entrainment; caffeine/adenosine; CBT-I stimulus control
- **Evidence:** A/B tools (A); founder packaging (B/C)
- **Population studied:** Sleep science / insomnia CBT; entrepreneur sleep–ideation studies (not this exact bundle RCT)
- **Limitations:** Does not manufacture hours; crisis adherence hard
- **Contraindications:** DIY aggressive sleep restriction for insomnia disorder — clinician
- **Safety:** Not treatment for apnea or mood disorders
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Coach/EA helpful; clinician if insomnia disorder
- **Source:** AASM behavioral insomnia guidance; PMC2656292 sleep deprivation review; entrepreneur sleep literature (Gish/Williamson line); caffeine timing sleep research
- **Creator:** Public CBT-I/circadian components bundled
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Pitch-Week Physiological Sigh + Affect Label + If–Then

- **Protocol:** Pitch-Week Physiological Sigh + Affect Label + If–Then
- **Client type:** Startup founders
- **Specific situation:** 30–60 min before investor pitch / demo day
- **Problem:** Acute sympathetic spike + catastrophic cognition
- **When:** Pre-pitch; also usable post-rejection spike
- **Duration:** 3–6 min
- **Frequency:** Per high-stakes pitch
- **Instructions:** 1) 1–3 physiological sighs (double nasal inhale, long exhale) or 5-min cyclic sighing if time. 2) Quiet affect label: “I notice anxiety/excitement.” 3) One if–then: “If I blank, then pause, water, restart slide X.” 4) Process-image opening 60s only — not outcome fantasy.
- **Immediate effect:** Lower respiratory arousal; clearer first actions
- **Longer-term:** Stable pre-performance routine
- **Mechanism:** Exhale-emphasized autonomic shift; affect labeling; implementation intentions
- **Evidence:** B+A components; package C — Balban Cell Rep Med 2023 (PubMed 36630953); Gollwitzer II (A); Lieberman affect labeling (B)
- **Population studied:** Healthy adults (sigh RCT); II broad; not founder-only RCT
- **Limitations:** Won’t fix unrehearsed content
- **Contraindications:** Breath-focus panic — skip to label + if–then
- **Safety:** Stop if dizzy
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Optional
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/ ; II literature; affect labeling literature
- **Creator:** Composite public protocols
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Entrepreneurial 3Rs Micro-Dose (Respite–Reappraisal–Regimen)

- **Protocol:** Entrepreneurial 3Rs Micro-Dose (Respite–Reappraisal–Regimen)
- **Client type:** Startup founders / entrepreneurs
- **Specific situation:** Weekly prevention of entrepreneurial ill-being
- **Problem:** No detachment; exhaustion; insomnia risk
- **When:** Scheduled ≥3×/week elements
- **Duration:** Respite 10–20 min; reappraisal 5–10; regimen daily rails
- **Frequency:** Weekly structure
- **Instructions:** RESPITE: phone-away walk/music without work talk. REAPPRAISAL: worst/best/most-likely for one stressor OR cognitive reappraisal. REGIMEN: fixed wake; aerobic 20–40 min most days; one non-work social contact.
- **Immediate effect:** Agency; brief mood lift
- **Longer-term:** Framework predicts better well-being; RCTs still limited
- **Mechanism:** Effort-recovery detachment; reappraisal; circadian/exercise regimen
- **Evidence:** B conceptual profession-specific (Williamson, Gish & Stephan 2021 ET&P doi:10.1177/10422587211006431) + A/B components; package C until founder RCTs
- **Population studied:** Entrepreneurs (framework); components general pops
- **Limitations:** Not a fully validated multi-arm founder RCT package
- **Contraindications:** Severe depression → clinical care
- **Safety:** Don’t use reappraisal to deny real runway risk
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Peer group/coach ideal
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/10422587211006431
- **Creator:** Williamson, Gish, Stephan (framework)
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Post-Rejection Cognitive Defusion + Tiny Behavioral Activation

- **Protocol:** Post-Rejection Cognitive Defusion + Tiny Behavioral Activation
- **Client type:** Startup founders
- **Specific situation:** After investor pass / lost customer
- **Problem:** Shame spiral, rumination, withdrawal
- **When:** Within ~2h post-rejection
- **Duration:** 10–20 min
- **Frequency:** As needed
- **Instructions:** 1) Defusion: “I’m having the thought that I’m a failure.” 2) Affect label. 3) Tiny BA: 2–10 min walk / tidy / message one peer. 4) Schedule 15-min worry window tomorrow — postpone now. 5) No alcohol as primary downshift.
- **Immediate effect:** Interrupts fusion/withdrawal
- **Longer-term:** Rejection-resilience repertoire
- **Mechanism:** ACT defusion; BA anti-rumination; worry postpone
- **Evidence:** A/C — BA strong; brief defusion C; situation C
- **Population studied:** Clinical/general ACT & BA; not founder-specific RCT
- **Limitations:** Not therapy for major depression/trauma
- **Contraindications:** Suicidality → emergency care
- **Safety:** Wellness only
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Therapist if persistent
- **Source:** BA evidence base; ACT skills; CBT postpone-worry
- **Creator:** Public psych skills bundle
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Founder Decision Batch + Peak-Hour Protection

- **Protocol:** Founder Decision Batch + Peak-Hour Protection
- **Client type:** Startup founders
- **Specific situation:** Daily decision clutter
- **Problem:** Attentional depletion / decision thrash
- **When:** Morning 5–10 min planning
- **Duration:** 5–10 min plan + all-day structure
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Instructions:** 1) Sort decisions: today / delegate / park. 2) Irreversible calls only after sleep + food. 3) Batch similars. 4) If–then: “If Slack not urgent, then check :00 and :30 only.”
- **Immediate effect:** Less thrash
- **Longer-term:** Higher quality on what matters
- **Mechanism:** Attentional conservation; II; ego-depletion model contested — use as scheduling heuristic
- **Evidence:** C (II component A); decision-fatigue field evidence mixed — label honestly
- **Population studied:** II general; healthcare decision-fatigue reviews mixed
- **Limitations:** Not license to avoid hard calls
- **Contraindications:** None specific
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Coach optional
- **Source:** Gollwitzer II; decision-fatigue systematic reviews (mixed)
- **Creator:** Practice heuristic + II
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
