### IC-Day Judgment Hygiene (Sleep Gate + Light + Pre-Vote Downshift)

- **Protocol:** IC-Day Judgment Hygiene (Sleep Gate + Light + Pre-Vote Downshift)
- **Client type:** VC / PE partners
- **Specific situation:** Investment committee vote day after travel or late diligence
- **Problem:** Fatigue-skewed risk perception / confidence miscalibration
- **When:** Morning of IC through vote
- **Duration:** Rails + 5 min pre-vote
- **Frequency:** Per IC
- **Instructions:** 1) Protect ≥6.5–7.5h opportunity night before when possible; if short sleep, flag “fatigue state” on memo. 2) Morning outdoor light. 3) Irreversible votes not immediately after red-eye without delay when process allows. 4) T-5: 3 physiological sighs + affect label (“urgency/FOMO”) + reread kill-criteria checklist. 5) No celebratory alcohol before night sleep post-IC.
- **Immediate effect:** Slightly cleaner calibration
- **Longer-term:** Culture of cognitive state awareness
- **Mechanism:** Sleep loss impairs complex decision & risk (Killgore/Harrison–Horne lines); arousal regulation; checklist as cognitive offload
- **Evidence:** B/C — sleep–decision science B; IC packaging C; no VC-specific RCT
- **Population studied:** Sleep deprivation cognition; aviation/medical analogs; not IC RCTs
- **Limitations:** Deal timing pressures conflict
- **Contraindications:** None for hygiene; don’t overclaim certainty
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Firm process helps
- **Source:** PMC2656292; Killgore decision/sleep work; Balban sigh
- **Creator:** Profession packaging
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Portfolio Contagion Reset (Defusion + Boundary Call Script)

- **Protocol:** Portfolio Contagion Reset (Defusion + Boundary Call Script)
- **Client type:** VC / PE
- **Specific situation:** After crisis call with panicked founder/CEO
- **Problem:** Emotional contagion → reactive meddling or avoidance
- **When:** Immediately post-call
- **Duration:** 8–15 min
- **Frequency:** As needed
- **Instructions:** 1) Defusion: “I am catching their panic; it is data + emotion.” 2) Affect label. 3) Write: facts / fears / next one action. 4) If–then: “If urge to send reactive Slack <10 min, then wait 30 min or draft only.” 5) Optional 5 min resonance breathing.
- **Immediate effect:** Less reactive outreach
- **Longer-term:** Better boardcraft under fire
- **Mechanism:** Emotional contagion interruption; ACT defusion; II delay
- **Evidence:** C package; components B/A
- **Population studied:** ACT/skills general; PE/VC essays on cognitive load (practice)
- **Limitations:** Not a substitute for deal skills
- **Contraindications:** None
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** Partner peer optional
- **Source:** ACT skills; II; WEF/TallRock-style cognitive asset essays (practice, not RCT)
- **Creator:** Composite
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### Conference Gauntlet Circadian Survival

- **Protocol:** Conference Gauntlet Circadian Survival
- **Client type:** VC / PE
- **Specific situation:** Multi-day conference (Milken / SuperReturn / India Week class)
- **Problem:** Cumulative sleep + alcohol + networking arousal
- **When:** Conference week
- **Duration:** Daily micro-rails
- **Frequency:** Each conference
- **Instructions:** Fixed wake; morning light; alcohol cap rule (e.g., ≤1 or none on night before IC-like meetings); 10–20 min nap early afternoon if needed; 5 min sigh/PMR before key dinner; hydrate; one non-network meal alone.
- **Immediate effect:** Less day-3 collapse
- **Longer-term:** Conferences less extractive
- **Mechanism:** Sleep + alcohol fragmentation; arousal management
- **Evidence:** B/C applied sleep/alcohol science
- **Population studied:** Travelers; not conference-RCT for VCs
- **Limitations:** Social norms
- **Contraindications:** None
- **Safety:** OK
- **Self-admin?:** Yes
- **Supervision?:** EA logistics
- **Source:** Sleep alcohol fragmentation literature; CDC travel tips
- **Creator:** Situation package
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

### LP Roadshow Jet-Lag Mini (Non-Adapt vs Adapt Choice)

- **Protocol:** LP Roadshow Jet-Lag Mini (Non-Adapt vs Adapt Choice)
- **Client type:** PE / VC fundraisers
- **Specific situation:** Short multi-city LP sprint
- **Problem:** Micro jet lag without recovery days
- **When:** Roadshow
- **Duration:** Trip-length plan
- **Frequency:** Per roadshow
- **Instructions:** If city <3 nights: stay on home time when feasible (CDC non-adapt). If longer: shift with light/melatonin plan. Protect sleep opportunity before key LP meeting; sighs pre-meeting; no late alcohol.
- **Immediate effect:** More predictable alertness windows
- **Longer-term:** Less fundraise burnout
- **Mechanism:** Circadian strategy choice
- **Evidence:** A/B CDC jet lag
- **Population studied:** Travelers
- **Limitations:** LP calendars dominate
- **Contraindications:** Melatonin with physician if needed
- **Safety:** As jet-lag protocol
- **Self-admin?:** Mostly
- **Supervision?:** Travel medicine optional
- **Source:** CDC Yellow Book 2026 jet lag
- **Creator:** CDC-aligned adaptation
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
