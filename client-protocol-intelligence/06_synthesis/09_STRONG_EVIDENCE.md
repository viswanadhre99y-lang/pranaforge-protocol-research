## Strong negative evidence (doctrines to enforce)

| Do not claim | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| NSDR replaces sleep hour-for-hour | Not supported; local-sleep EEG ≠ whole-organism sleep homeostasis |
| Sleep hygiene alone cures chronic insomnia | AASM: insufficient as monotherapy |
| High-dose melatonin is better | CDC: >5 mg not recommended; timing > dose heroics |
| Wrong-timed light helps jet lag | Can worsen misalignment |
