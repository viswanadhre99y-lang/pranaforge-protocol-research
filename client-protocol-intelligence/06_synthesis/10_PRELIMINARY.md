## Client-specific evidence gaps (preliminary by construction)

Occupation-specific RCTs for UHNW hosts, power couples, executive parents protocol packs, and competitive exam PPR stacks are thin — we **transfer** from adjacent literatures (sport psych, CBT-I, circadian, BA) and tag as lifestyle/situation adaptations.
