# 11 — TRADITIONAL / EXPERIENTIAL (D) — Honest Labeling

**Compiled:** 2026-09-19 (Asia/Calcutta)

Traditional practices appear in Cluster D because clients (especially India UHNW, faith-adjacent, and optimizer-curious) already use them. Grade **D** means: available with cultural fit and consent — **not** upgraded to A/B science marketing.

| Practice | Traditional frame | Modern evidence posture | Cluster D touchpoints | Product rule |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| Alternate nostril (nadi shodhana) | Yogic nadi / prana balance | Small autonomic studies; **hemisphere-balance claims unsupported (E)** | Performance seekers, exam candidates wanting "yogic edge" | Offer as traditional paced-breath variant; no mystical neuroscience |
| Broader pranayama families | Classical Hatha/Raja | Heterogeneous; often collapses into slow/exhale breathing effects | Travel wind-down; student evening practice | Prefer cyclic sighing / resonance when claiming science |
| Yoga Nidra sankalpa | Yogic resolve/intention | Psychologically ≈ goal priming; not metaphysical causation | NSDR scripts for travelers/students | Optional; never LOA |
| Autogenic formulas | Schultz clinical tradition | C-tier mixed signals in meta-analyses | Sleep seekers who dislike active PMR | Optional traditional self-relaxation |
| Faith / contemplative practices | Client's own religion | May support coping/meaning; not our IP | Faith leaders (additional type); some families | Never colonize faith as a "protocol stack" |
| Royal / protocol household rituals | Etiquette + security culture | Ops constraint, not science | high_profile_families; royal/protocol additional type | Design *around* ritual calendars |
| Ayurveda dinacharya light/meal heuristics | Classical daily regimen | Overlaps modern zeitgeber ideas unevenly | India travelers/hosts | Lifestyle analogy OK; don't invent clinical Ayurveda claims here |
| Cold exposure as tapas / hormesis folklore | Ascetic / modern influencer blend | Acute arousal tool possible; **trauma-rewire claims E** | Optimizers, burnout curiosity | Cold face as optional peak tool only; medical cautions |

## Tradition ≠ evidence upgrade

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| "Traditional practice; modern evidence limited" | "Ancient science proves hemisphere balance" |
| Client opts into culturally meaningful script | Forcing sankalpa / prayer language on secular clients |
| Pair traditional breath with A/B rails (light, sleep) | Replacing CBT-I path with pranayama for clinical insomnia |
