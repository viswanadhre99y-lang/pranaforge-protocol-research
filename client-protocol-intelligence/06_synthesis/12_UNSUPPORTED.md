## Language hygiene

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Proven to cure jet lag | Timed light (± melatonin) associated with faster adaptation |
| Replaces sleep | May aid rest/relaxation; not a sleep substitute |
| Detox / alkaline | Changes arousal/CO2/attention (specific) |
| Manifests results | Improves preparation/motivation when paired with action |
