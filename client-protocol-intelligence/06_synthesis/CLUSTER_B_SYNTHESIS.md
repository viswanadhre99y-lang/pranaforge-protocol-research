## Distinctive findings by client
- **Management Consultants** (`management_consultants`): Consulting literature emphasizes **organizational support + workload/WLB** over apps; detachment rituals and travel sleep hygiene are highest-ROI individual layers. White-space: consulting-specific brief protocols validated in MBB samples remain scarce. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Lawyers / Attorneys** (`lawyers`): Lawyer-specific mindfulness curricula (Anxious Lawyer / Mindful Pause) are a rare **profession-specific** evidence pocket. Detachment is hard when hyper-aroused — pair boundary rituals with cognitive skills. White-space: large preregistered RCTs still limited. _(n=22 protocols)_
- **Physicians & Surgeons** (`physicians_surgeons`): Strongest **profession-specific performance** evidence among clusters (surgical MSC; pre-sim breathing). Burnout: individual effects small–moderate; **coaching** signal for physicians; label honestly vs org reform. White-space: live OR RCTs of ultra-brief breath still limited. _(n=23 protocols)_
- **Dentists** (`dentists`): Dentists: **MSD/ergonomics** is the distinctive high-evidence profession layer. Self-compassion for burnout is promising white-space. Performance anxiety shares generic breath tools. _(n=22 protocols)_
- **Psychologists / Therapists** (`psychologists_therapists`): Therapists need **between-session residue management + supervision boundaries** more than more meditation apps. Evidence for self-care is promising but mixed; white-space: brief between-session RCTs. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Software Engineers** (`software_engineers`): Highest-ROI profession layer is **boundary/detachment + insomnia rails** (iCBT-I–informed recovery training evidence). White-space: engineer-only RCTs still limited; borrow blurred-boundary worker trials. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Academics / Researchers** (`academics_researchers`): Academics: **evaluative threat + writing isolation** dominate. Structured mindfulness programs have better evidence than random apps; pair with BA for writing. White-space: faculty-specific (not only student) RCTs. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Sales Professionals** (`sales_professionals`): Sales: **rejection recovery + irrational target beliefs** are the distinctive layer (REBT trial). Pair with BA to keep pipeline behavior moving. White-space: larger preregistered sales RCTs. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Real Estate Professionals** (`real_estate_professionals`): Real estate: **always-on availability** is the core lifestyle pathology. Highest-ROI is boundary/coverage systems + detachment; occupation-specific trials are white-space. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Journalists / Media** (`journalists_media`): Journalists: **trauma-informed pathways** beat generic wellness. Distinctive protocol is peer support + specialist referral, not more breathwork alone. White-space: strong journalism-specific intervention RCTs still scarce. _(n=21 protocols)_
- **Architects / Designers** (`architects_designers`): Architects/designers: distinctive stack is **MSD microbreaks + creative attention restoration**; occupation-specific trials are white-space — honest label + borrow adjacent evidence. _(n=21 protocols)_

## Cross-cutting patterns
1. **A/B defaults travel well:** cyclic sighing, coherent breathing, stimulus control (clinician-framed), light timing, implementation intentions, reappraisal, detachment.
2. **Profession-specific pockets are uneven:** strongest in surgery (MSC), law (mindfulness curricula), dentistry (ergonomics), sales (REBT), physicians (coaching meta-signals).
3. **White-space:** consulting-validated micro-protocols; architect RCTs; journalism intervention RCTs; real-estate boundary trials; therapist between-session micro-RCTs.
4. **Burnout honesty:** individual skills are complementary; org workload/culture reforms dominate causal pathway — especially medicine and consulting.
5. **Safety:** CBT-I restriction never auto-prescribed; trauma pathways for journalists/therapists; substance escalation for law.
