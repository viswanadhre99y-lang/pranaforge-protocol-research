## What this is NOT

- **Not** PranaForge proprietary IP (not Daily Forge / Emotional Load / Clarity / Stress-Field recipes)

- **Not** permission to DIY aggressive CBT-I sleep restriction or mega-dose melatonin
- **Not** a claim that **NSDR replaces sleep**

## Hard product rules (travel / sleep)

1. **Light** is the primary jet-lag zeitgeber; timing depends on direction and zones (CDC Yellow Book 2026).
2. **Melatonin** is a timed chronobiotic — typically **0.5–1 mg** may suffice; **avoid >5 mg** DIY mega-dosing; wrong timing worsens misalignment; product quality varies.
3. **NSDR / Yoga Nidra** = deep rest adjunct — **not** physiological sleep replacement.
4. **CBT-I** stimulus control / sleep restriction = clinician-guided framing for clinical insomnia.
5. Never market E-tier claims (detox breath, LOA manifestation, hemisphere-balancing nostril myths).
