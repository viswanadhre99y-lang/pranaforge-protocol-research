# Personalized message engine

## Formula
`[Observed context] + [Why now] + [Single action] + [Duration] + [Optional skip]`

## Examples
| Bad | Better |
|-----|--------|
| Remember to breathe | Three meetings stacked. 3 minutes before your next call — exhale-heavy reset. |
| Time to recover! | Short sleep last night + travel tomorrow. Tonight: wind-down starts 90 min before bed. |
| Stay positive | Pitch in 25 minutes. Process cue only — first 60 seconds of your open. |

## Tone by receptivity
- Low time: ≤12 words + deep link  
- Medium: 1–2 sentences  
- High / evening: slightly longer stack OK  

## Anti-patterns
- Guilt (“you failed your streak”)  
- Fake certainty (“your HRV shows anxiety”)  
- Medical claims  
- Manifestation metaphysics as science  
- Firehoses (>3 unsolicited pushes/day default cap)

## Channels (PranaForge)
1. Principal app / WhatsApp **opt-in**  
2. Staff console suggestion (human delivers) — preferred for UHNW Phase-1  
3. In-room tablet during hotel activation  

## Timing research hooks
Fogg triggers, habit discontinuity, implementation intentions in message CTA (“If elevator → 3 sighs”).
