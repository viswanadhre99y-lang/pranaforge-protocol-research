# Privacy, consent, safety

## Consent layers (must be separable)
1. Calendar free/busy  
2. Calendar event titles  
3. Approximate location / geofence  
4. Wearable sleep & HR  
5. Push notifications  
6. Staff visibility of state  

Revocation must work within product SLAs.

## Jurisdictions (PranaForge India-first)
- India **DPDP Act 2023** — consent, purpose limitation, significant data fiduciary duties if scale thresholds met  
- GDPR if EU clients  
- Health-ish data: treat wearable + mental-state self-report as sensitive  

## Ethical red lines
- No covert inference of mental illness  
- No secret location tracking  
- No selling client state to third parties  
- Clear retention windows  

## Safety / escalation
| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Explicit crisis language | Show local emergency resources; notify designated human if consented; **no protocol playbook** |
| Extreme sleep deprivation + safety-critical role (pilot/surgeon) | Advise professional channels; do not “hack performance” |
| Eating disorder / self-harm content | Escalation path only |
| Orthosomnia / tracker obsession | Reduce wearable-driven messaging |

## Wellness ≠ medicine
System may support regulation, sleep *hygiene*, travel timing, performance routines.  
Must not claim to treat disorders, cure disease, or replace CBT-I clinicians for insomnia disorder.
