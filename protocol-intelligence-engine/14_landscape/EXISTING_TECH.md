# Landscape — JITAI, APIs, companies, gaps

## Academic lineage
- **JITAI** — Nahum-Shani et al., Ann Behav Med 2018 (components; decision points; tailoring)
- **MRT** — Klasnja et al.; Murphy lab methods for causal proximal effects
- **HeartSteps** — activity JITAI exemplar
- **Receptivity** — Künzler, Mishra et al. (state-of-receptivity ≠ interruptibility)
- **Wearable sleep validation** — Chinoy et al. Sleep 2021; Miller et al. Sensors 2022 (stages limited)

## Consumer / wellness
| Product | Overlap | Gap vs PIE |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Calm / Headspace | Content library | Weak real-time selection + UHNW staff loop |
| WHOOP / Oura / Apple | Sleep/strain signals | Vendor composites; not protocol decision OS |
| Elite HRV / Welltory | HRV biofeedback | Narrow modality |
| Reflectly / journaling apps | Self-report | No evidence-graded protocol ranking |

## Performance / clinical-adjacent
| Product | Overlap | Gap |
|---------|---------|-----|
| CBT-I digital therapeutics | Sleep protocols | Clinical scope; not travel/pitch moments |
| AMS / sports psych tools | PPR, imagery | Not calendar-JITAI for executives |
| Muse / breath apps | Guided sessions | Limited context fusion |

## APIs / building blocks
- Google/Microsoft Calendar  
- HealthKit / Health Connect / vendor partner APIs (sleep duration)  
- Push (APNs/FCM); WhatsApp Business (consent-heavy)  
- Rules: OPA/JSONLogic; ranker as pure function for testability  

## Gap PIE targets
1. **Evidence-graded catalog × explainable hybrid ranker** (not black-box coach)  
2. **Silence-first JITAI** for UHNW  
3. **Staff-in-loop console** as product surface  
4. **CPI moments** (pitch, landing, conflict) wired to protocols  
5. **Honest uncertainty** (measured vs estimated vs self-report)  
6. **Vault IP firewall** (opaque IDs)

None of the above is “another meditation pack.”
