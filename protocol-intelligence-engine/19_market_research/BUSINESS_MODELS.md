**Date:** 2026-09-19 Asia/Calcutta  
**Question:** What to sell, to whom, at what price — B2C vs B2B vs B2B2C vs hybrid — with unit economics sketches. All prices **[A]** unless noted.

## 1. Model comparison

| Model | Who pays | Who uses | Pros | Cons | Phase-1 fit |
|-------|----------|----------|------|------|-------------|
| **B2B SaaS seats** | Retreat/hotel/FO | Staff | Matches product; clearer WTP path | Small TAM | **Best** |
| **B2B2C** | Property; guest sees thin UX | Staff + optional guest | Brand halo | Scope creep; guest support | Later |
| **B2C subscription** | UHNW individual | Principal | Huge narrative | Competes with Calm; trust; CAC | **Poor Y1** |
| **Hybrid desk** | FO retainer + SaaS | Lifestyle team | High ACV | Sales-heavy; privacy | Strong for S2 |
| **Usage / per recommendation** | Ops budget | Staff | Aligns to value | Unpredictable; resists | Weak |
| **Implementation + SaaS** | Capex + opex | Staff | Funds customization | Services drag | Useful early |
| **White-label license** | Brand digital | Many sites | Scale | Long sales; SLAs | P1–P2 |
| **Clinical partnership rev-share** | Clinic | Clinicians | Big tickets | MDSW; out of Phase 1 | No |

**Recommendation:** Lead **B2B SaaS (+ light implementation)**. Optionally **hybrid FO desk**. Defer B2C. Keep B2B2C as upsell only when staff loop proven.

## 3. Unit economics (illustrative base case **[A]**)

### Assumptions
- Blended ACV $28k  
- Gross margin SaaS 75% after hosting/support  
- CAC (founder-led sales) $8k (travel + time)  
- Payback target < 12 months  
- Logo churn 20% / yr early  

### Sketch
| Metric | Value |
|--------|------:|
| Year-1 contribution per logo | $28k × 0.75 − $8k ≈ **$13k** |
| Steady-state LTV (3 yr, 20% churn rough) | ~$28k × 0.75 × ~2.7 ≈ **$57k** |
| LTV/CAC | ~7× if CAC stays founder-led; **collapses** if enterprise SDR hired early |

**Implication:** Stay founder/concierge sales until ~15–20 logos; don’t buy ads into $6T wellness audiences.
