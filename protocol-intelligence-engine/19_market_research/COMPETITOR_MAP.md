## 6. Competitive dynamics

1. **Incumbent gravity:** Substitutes are free and social; PIE must sell *risk reduction + consistency*, not “AI.”  
2. **Content giants** win distribution; PIE should **not** fight on content volume.  
3. **Coaching platforms** win enterprise HR; PIE should win **hospitality + FO ops**.  
4. **Wearables** win sensors; partner later, don’t rebuild.  
5. **India retreat brands** may build internal tools — partner or white-label faster than displace.

## 7. Differentiation thesis (testable)

PIE wins if buyers believe:  
**“Explainable, safety-filtered, silence-aware protocol selection for staff — tied to our house catalog and client history — beats WhatsApp memory.”**

Falsifiers: buyers only want booking/POS; or refuse any AI; or demand diagnostic claims PIE won’t make.
