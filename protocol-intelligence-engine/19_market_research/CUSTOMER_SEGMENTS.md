## S1 — India luxury wellness retreats (P0)

### Who
Program directors, spa/wellness directors, senior therapists at Ananda / Vana / Atmantan / Amanbagh-class and peers. Guests pay premium for Ayurveda, breath, bodywork, mindfulness stacks.

### Evidence
- Kotak: 60%+ UHNI visited retreat in 3 years; wellness luxury inflation +14.3%/yr; some ₹2 lakh/night **[E]**
- Ananda/Vana/Atmantan package bands roughly $550–700+/person/night class **[E]**
- Mordor India wellness tourism ~$28B (2025) **[E]** — GMV context only

### Real workflow
Intake form → dosha/preferences interview → house protocol menu → therapist judgment → daily schedule → WhatsApp logistics → weak structured outcomes.

### Jobs PIE helps
- Consistent Top-3 given guest state + available minutes + contraindications  
- Train junior therapists to house standard  
- Audit “why this protocol”  
- Silence / rest days as first-class  

### Buyer / user / economic buyer
| Role | Interest |
|------|----------|
| Wellness director | User + champion |
| GM / owner | Economic buyer if RevPAR / NPS / repeat |
| Therapist | User; may resist “AI” |

### Willingness to pay (hypothesis **[A]**)
$1–5k/mo per property for staff seats + outcomes dashboard, if it reduces training time and increases perceived personalization.

### Risks
Change management; “our Ayurveda is unique” politics; pressure to add medical claims.

### 10 discovery questions
1. How do you decide today’s protocol stack for a guest in <5 minutes?  
2. What % of recommendations are copy-paste from yesterday?  
3. Where do contraindications live today?  
4. What happens when the senior therapist is off?  
5. Do you log outcomes beyond “guest smiled”?  
6. Would you pay for consistency or for guest-facing wow?  
7. WhatsApp: sacred or painful?  
8. Would silence recommendations feel valuable or lazy?  
9. Must the system speak Ayurveda language, evidence grades, or both?  
10. Who blocks software purchase — IT, medical advisor, owner?

## S2 — Family office / UHNW lifestyle desks (P0)

### Who
Lifestyle managers, private staff leads, FO principals’ chiefs of staff in Mumbai/Delhi/Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Chennai corridors. ~300 India FOs **[E]**; ~20k UHNWIs **[E]**.

### Evidence
- Lifestyle managers charge ~$150k/yr externally; in-house $120–300k+ **[E]**  
- Capgemini: advisors want AI for routine so humans focus on relationships **[E]** (wealth mgmt parallel)  
- UHNI 10% spend on health/wellness; 81% increased spend **[E]**

### Real workflow
Calendar of principal → travel → WhatsApp to trainer/coach/chef → ad-hoc recovery suggestions → notes in phone.

### Jobs PIE helps
- Moment-aware suggestions (pre-board meeting, post-flight) for the *desk*  
- Client_id isolation across principals/family members  
- Handoff between trainer / breath coach / travel nurse (non-medical)  

### WTP hypothesis **[A]**
$1–5k/mo per household desk — cheap vs human labor if it prevents one bad travel week.

### Risks
Privacy/multiparty ACL not built; principals hate notifications; trust of “AI.”

### 10 discovery questions
1. How many principals does one lifestyle lead cover?  
2. What recovery protocols are already standardized?  
3. Calendar access — allowed?  
4. Wearables — used or ignored?  
5. Preferred delivery: staff whisper vs WhatsApp to principal?  
6. Budget line: wellness, security, or ops?  
7. Ever hired a digital tool for this? Why fired?  
8. Non-negotiable: discretion language?  
9. Family members sharing state — OK?  
10. Success metric: principal energy, fewer flare-ups, staff hours saved?

## S3 — Luxury hotel spa desks (P1)

### Who
Spa managers at 5★ / luxury brands (Oberoi ASMI, ITC Kaya Kalp, Taj/J Wellness, Leela, etc.). India ~823 five-star hotels **[E]**; serious program spas are a minority.

### Evidence
- Brand wellness frameworks expanding (e.g., Oberoi ASMI five-pillar) **[E]**  
- Spa market ~$3.9B India **[E]**  
- Spa often 3–8% of hotel ancillary (industry rule-of-thumb **[A]** — confirm per property)

### Workflow
Booking sheet → therapist skill menu → upsell package → limited longitudinal guest profile across stays.

### Jobs PIE helps
- Menu intelligence by guest context (jet lag, wedding stress, short gap)  
- Cross-property consistency for brands  

### WTP hypothesis **[A]**
$500–2k/mo per spa; brand deal $25–80k/yr.

### Risks
Procurement; PMS/spa software incumbents; low digital maturity.

### 10 discovery questions
1. Average treatment decision time?  
2. % repeat guests with known preferences?  
3. Integration needs (Opera, SpaSoft, ThinkSPAIN)?  
4. Therapist utilization vs personalization tradeoff?  
5. Would Top-3 help upsell duration/dose?  
6. Brand vs property decision rights?  
7. Training budget last FY?  
8. Guest complaint themes?  
9. Interest in post-stay micro-protocols (non-medical)?  
10. Data residency requirements?

## S4 — Urban premium recovery studios (P2)

Breathwork, ice/heat, yoga therapy boutiques in metros. High density, low ACV, fast feedback.

**Fit:** Good for learning loops; weak for UHNW brand. Use as **experiment sandboxes**, not core SOM.

### 10 questions
1. Sessions/day? 2. Staff turnover? 3. Protocol count? 4. Contraindication process? 5. App already used? 6. WTP ceiling? 7. Franchise ambitions? 8. Outcome tracking? 9. WhatsApp booking share? 10. Medical referrals?

## S5 — Corporate executive wellness non-clinical (P2)

CHROs / People Ops buying resilience for CXO bands. Overlaps BetterUp / Headspace Health.

**Caution:** Easy to slide into mental-health clinical claims; keep to performance micro-protocols + staff coaches.

### 10 questions
1. Coach vs app budget split? 2. CXO participation rates? 3. Privacy concerns? 4. Procurement cycle? 5. Success KPIs? 6. Existing EAP? 7. Travel-heavy exec %? 8. India-only vs global? 9. Would staff console for internal coaches work? 10. Medical/legal review triggers?

## S7 / S8 — Direct consumer (Avoid as primary Y1)

UHNW consumers pay for **humans and places**, not another mindfulness app (Kotak experience shift **[E]**). Mass consumers have Calm/Headspace/Wysa. PIE’s differentiation (explainable ranker + silence + staff loop) is diluted in B2C feed.
