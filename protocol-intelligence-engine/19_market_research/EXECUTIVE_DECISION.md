# EXECUTIVE_DECISION — PranaForge PIE Market Research

**Date:** 2026-09-19 Asia/Calcutta  
**Audience:** Founder (Viswanadh Reddy)  
**Product reality:** Staff Top-3 sandbox; safety; outcomes; client_id isolation; soft golden **78–84.5%**; integrations stubbed; **not clinical**; India-first non-medical Phase 1.

---

## 12-bullet digest

1. **PIE today is a B2B staff decision tool**, not a consumer wellness app and not a medical device — market strategy must match that shape.  
2. **Macro $6.8T (GWI/Global Wellness Institute) / ~$2T (McKinsey) wellness is context only** — never PranaForge TAM; bottom-up India software attach is roughly **$4–14M TAM** with **~$0.5–2.2M SAM** and **~$75k–$1.2M SOM Y3** across scenarios.  
3. **Demand evidence is strong for luxury wellness spend in India** (Kotak: wellness inflation +14.3%/yr; 81% UHNI increased spend; retreats at ₹2L/night) — but that is spend on *experiences/humans*, not proof of software WTP.  
4. **Supply-side wealth pool supports a niche premium play:** ~19.9k India UHNWIs (Knight Frank 2026) and ~390k HNWIs (Capgemini); ~300 family offices — thin, rich, relationship-sold.  
5. **Best beachheads:** (S1) program-led luxury retreats and (S2) FO/lifestyle desks; hotel spas (S3) as expansion; longevity/concierge clinics **out of Phase-1 boundary**.  
6. **Dominant competitor is a substitute:** WhatsApp + human + notes — not Calm; beat it on consistency, safety, handoff, audit, silence.  
7. **Sell B2B SaaS (+ light implementation)**; optional FO desk hybrid; **do not lead B2C**; B2B2C only after staff proof.  
8. **Moat is prospective:** workflow embedding + house catalog/vault firewall + decision audit + per-client response graphs — soft golden is an engineering KPI, not a moat.  
9. **Missing evidence that matters most:** paid WTP, outcome-log adherence, whether context tags change decisions enough to justify integrations.  
10. **Regulatory knife-edge:** stay non-MDSW under CDSCO 2026 software guidance via claims hygiene; take DPDP seriously for UHNW data; run counsel Exp-H before press.  
11. **What NOT to build now:** consumer meditation farm, autonomous principal spam, fake wearables, clinical longevity OS, bandits before outcome volume, fundraising theater off $6T slides.  
12. **Decision:** Run discovery + **paid pilots on Wedges A and B** in parallel; use D as a low-risk side door; park F/G; delay calendar build until Exp-C passes — **no claim that any wedge “will work.”**

---

## Top 3 wedges by evidence (with caveats)

### 1) Wedge A — Retreat Staff Top-3 Copilot
- **Why ranked #1 on evidence fit:** Shipped product = staff console; India retreat demand and personalization race evidenced (Kotak, retreat pricing); non-medical fit clean.  
- **Caveats:** Software WTP unproven; therapist change management; “Ayurveda isn’t rankable” objection; seasonal hospitality buying.  
- **Falsifier:** Exp-B ceiling <$400/mo or pilots without outcome logging.

### 2) Wedge B — Family Office Recovery Desk
- **Why #2:** UHNW/FO growth evidenced; human lifestyle costs ($120–300k+) make even $24–60k ACV rational **if** trust clears; moment library maps to founder CPI.  
- **Caveats:** Multiparty ACL not built; relationship-only sales; DPDP blast radius; n of FOs ~300 limits scale.  
- **Falsifier:** Principals reject any AI assist (Exp-J) or security questionnaire blocks SaaS.

### 3) Wedge C — Luxury Hotel Spa Gap-Fit *(edge over D on scale optionality)*
- **Why #3:** Large property count (~823 five-stars); brand wellness frameworks expanding (e.g., Oberoi ASMI); dose/modality features already in Phase 2.  
- **Caveats:** Spa software budgets often tiny; PMS incumbents; weaker pain than program retreats; risk of being “nice iPad toy.”  
- **Falsifier:** Cannot find buyer who owns budget beyond treatment upsell spreadsheets.

**Honorable mention:** Wedge D (Academy) — strongest *regulatory/sales ease*, weakest flywheel; use as land-and-expand aid, not core SOM.

## Market-driven architecture (do)

- Staff-speed UX, decision_record export, silence, source_class honesty  
- House catalog CMS + opaque vault IDs  
- DPDP deletion/export paths before FO scale  
- WhatsApp **staff** assist after UI pilot — not autonomous guest bots  
- Keep ranker hybrid/explainable  

## Architecture (don’t)

- Consumer notification brain  
- Medical mode toggle  
- Fake calendar/wearable demos  
- Population “efficacy” marketing from ratings

## Final line

India’s UHNW luxury wellness **wallet** is real and growing; PranaForge PIE’s **software invoice** is still a hypothesis. The rigorous move is beachhead B2B staff intelligence for retreats/desks, priced against human inconsistency — and to kill or pivot on experiment thresholds, not on wellness TAM mythology.
