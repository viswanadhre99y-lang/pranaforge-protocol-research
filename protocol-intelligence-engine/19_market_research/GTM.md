# GTM — Go-To-Market (PIE)

**Date:** 2026-09-19 Asia/Calcutta  
**Beachhead:** India luxury wellness retreats + FO lifestyle desks; non-medical staff console.

---

## 1. GTM principles

1. **Sell the job, not AI** — consistency, safety, handoff, training.  
2. **Staff-first demos** on real intake scenarios (use 110 golden-style cases as theater).  
3. **No fake wearables** in sales — trust asset.  
4. **Founder-led** until 15 logos.  
5. **WhatsApp for sales & success** — match buyer channels.  
6. Never lead with GWI $6T slide.

---

## 2. ICP (ideal customer profile) — Y1

**Must-have**
- India-based luxury retreat or UHNW desk  
- ≥3 staff who choose protocols daily  
- Champion wellness director or lifestyle lead  
- Willing to log outcomes for 6 weeks  
- Accepts non-medical positioning in writing  

**Disqualifiers**
- Demands diagnostic/treatment claims  
- Single therapist with no handoff pain  
- “Just white-label Calm” requests  
- Unwilling to put PIN/auth on staff tool  

---

## 3. Funnel

```
Warm intros (owners, spa consultants, FO RMs)
  → 30-min job interview (not pitch)
  → 45-min live Top-3 demo on their scenarios
  → 6-week paid pilot ($3–8k)
  → Success review (usage + ratings + qualitative)
  → Annual contract
```

**Conversion hypotheses [A]:** Interview→demo 50%; demo→pilot 30%; pilot→ACV 40%.

---

## 5. Messaging house

**Primary:** “Staff protocol intelligence — right micro-protocol, right dose, or silence — with an audit trail.”  
**Proof:** Soft golden on held-out scenarios; safety gateway; client isolation; Phase 2 response graph.  
**Boundary:** “Not a medical device. Not a diagnosis engine. Not another meditation app.”  
**Enemy:** Inconsistent WhatsApp folklore — respectfully.

### Do / Don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Show SILENCE as feature | Claim HRV cures anxiety |
| Show explanation fields | Promise biomarker outcomes |
| Show crisis escalate path | Spam guest phones in demo |
| Use India moment language (board, wedding, red-eye) | Use only Silicon Valley biohacker tropes |

---

## 6. Sales assets to build

1. 10 anonymized scenario cards from Indian UHNW life  
2. One-pager non-medical boundary  
3. Security/DPDP FAQ  
4. Pilot success checklist  
5. ROI worksheet (training hours, handoff errors) — no fake clinical ROI  
6. Reference deck only after 2 logos  

---

## 7. Launch sequence (90 days)

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | 15 discovery calls S1/S2 |
| 3–4 | Refine ICP; pick 3 pilot candidates |
| 5–10 | Run 2 paid pilots in parallel |
| 11–12 | Convert or kill; write case narrative |
| 13+ | Expand to 1 hotel spa + 1 FO desk |

---

## 8. Partnerships

- **Complementary:** Wearables (read-only later), spa PMS, private banks (intro only)  
- **Avoid early:** Clinical hospital chains as design partners (boundary bleed)  
- **Content:** Do not partner as “another channel for Calm-like audio”
